# Non-destructive imaging options for sealed Collector's Editions

Hi. I'm not a Discord user and haven't previously been involved in the INSIDE ARG, but I came across the sticker hunt and had an idea that seemed worth passing along.

There are still sealed Collector's Editions whose stickers aren't accessible without disturbing the packaging. Industrial X-ray CT has already been used to identify features inside unopened collectibles, and there are terahertz groups doing non-destructive hidden-text work through layered materials. Since an INSIDE sticker only needs to give us a three-digit number and one of three large marks, it seems at least plausible that the problem is easier than producing a normal photograph of the sticker.

I did a bit of legwork and put together a shortlist of specific people/facilities, what equipment or relevant work they have, and the useful geographic matches with known sealed owners. There are some surprisingly good leads, particularly a commercial CT company that already scans sealed Pokémon products and a Rome terahertz group with hidden-text work and an existing University of Malta connection.

I've attached the notes rather than making everyone ask for the details one at a time.

I'm only contributing the idea/research/leads here. I'm not volunteering to contact owners or imaging facilities, but hopefully this makes it easy for anyone already coordinating the sticker hunt to decide whether it's worth pursuing.
