# Conjecture Lab: protocol and disposition rules

_Adopted 8 October 2026; companions: [methodology audit](conjecture-led-research-audit-2026-10-08.md), [first active sprint](conjecture-lab/2026-10-08-first-sprint.md), and [current evidence map](hypothesis-test-coverage-matrix.md)._

## Purpose

In a creative codebreaking project, exploratory hunches can become productive **before** an external authority or observation supports them. Treat a deliberately adopted assumption as a temporary instrument for deriving consequences, rather than as a weakly established fact. The project needs permission to try, not permission to misreport.

The canonical observations, provenance, machine model, historical negatives, and frozen predictions remain unchanged. This lab is a separate research workflow. It **does not supersede** the evidence map or change the evidence status of any hypothesis.

## Modes and permission

| Mode | Actions allowed | Claims permitted | Exit or transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DISCOVER** | Brainstorm, hypothesize outputs, guess transformations, backsolve from known targets, search dictionaries, look at pictures, select provisional parameters and invent alternatives | “This interesting candidate was found using X/Y/Z” | Choose a coherent version of a conjecture or discard |
| **DEVELOP** | **Assume a versioned conjecture is true** and derive several downstream consequences, even across unsupported links; run exact constrained enumeration, story walkthroughs, sensitivity and contradiction audits | “Conditional on A+B, C follows. A real solution would require D.” | Produce novel risky predictions, exact conditional contradictions, concrete artifact/consumer requirements or a documented lower-priority branch |
| **VALIDATE** | Source-native parameter recovery, frozen versus new physical observations, independent reader responses, exact matched controls | “The evidence supports/falsifies this particular fixed candidate with these limits” | Update canonical maps only with qualified evidence |

**No laundering rule:** Choosing a string because it resembles a target can never subsequently count as the independent prediction of that same target. Write *fitted*, *source-assisted recognition*, *after seeing answer*, or *blind/frozen* beside each decision.

**No blanket ban on speculation:** “An authorial cue was not recovered” is a description of missing support; it does not falsify an entire speculative theory. A concrete contradiction with physical sticker observations **does** reject the frozen candidate that predicted the contradictory symbol.

**No unlimited fishing:** Time-box exploratory branches by whether they produce novel conditional constraints, a more economical walkthrough or a fresh discriminating test. Record transformations tried, the number of variants, and negative results when feasible. An arbitrarily enlarged parameter space is a reason to lower evidential interpretation, not necessarily to prohibit thinking about it.

## Assumption stack

A conjecture must explicitly label every nontrivial link with one of:

- **OBSERVED:** physical sticker or source facts, versioned and cited;
- **HISTORICALLY ATTESTED:** a puzzle technique that someone used or suggested, not validation for this application;
- **DERIVED CONDITIONAL:** a mathematical implication of already listed assumptions;
- **BORROWED ANALOGY:** a Playdead/design analogy without a native binding instruction;
- **INVENTED BRIDGE:** parameter, endpoint, word order, reader, source size or story element supplied deliberately for exploration;
- **FALSIFIED FIXED VARIANT:** a prior fixed rule contradicted by a physical observation or exact source-specific negative; never erase this status by renaming it.

For a hypothesis involving source material, identify whether the purported endpoint existed at the proposed time, was directly in the original Collector's Edition, or is a later archival resource. Modern archive enumeration is not equivalent to a contemporary player receiving that list.

## Conjecture-card contract

A card records: stable ID/version; adopted hunch; source facts; assumptions (each tagged); provisional authorial/player walkthrough; what outputs and other artifacts the theory predicts; all candidate-fitting degrees of freedom; a worked conditional deduction; what counts as a fatal *exact* contradiction; what would merely make it unsupported; next unseen observation or independently checkable native artifact; and result/disposition. Mark a fitted and a frozen hypothesis distinctly.

A complete “puzzle story” may end with an explicitly labeled invented response. It is a *simulation of the intended solution* useful for finding constraints, not a claim that such a response ever existed.

## Promotion gate

An exploratory hit may be recorded as a **lead**, not an evidentiary success. Move a claim to the validated map only if either:

1. newly observed physical symbols contradict or support a previously frozen candidate, with selection timing and prediction coverage preserved; or
2. an independently authenticated source provides the decoding operation or verifiable output/reaction; or
3. an exact conditional impossibility is proved for a completely specified target/mechanism pairing (scope must be named), without pretending it refutes every related decoder.

A preserved historical original clue or result may be used to backsolve; any resulting candidate must then predict something else *not used to build it*. A fitted match plus further retrospective matches still does not supply an independent positive test.

## Research cadence and division of labor

Maintain at least two genuinely different conjectures concurrently, and always keep a “there is no further endgame” control alive. Prefer two or three fully developed end-to-end narratives over dozens of one-step hypotheses. Complete one optimistic development pass **before** the adversarial pass; premature negative-controls-as-design-restrictions inhibit the very idea generation this lab is meant to restore.

Separate a problem's **feasibility** from its **evidence**: missing historical bytes, lack of a current server, incomplete physical sample, or no source-fixed instruction are distinct blockers. Don't make “no source-fixed consumer” an excuse to avoid conditional thought, and don't make “maybe an old consumer existed” a reason to assert one.

On each run, retain a short record: what was assumed, what changed, what was deduced, what was actually checked, whether the result was precommitted, and what new evidence could alter it. Don't renumber or overwrite historical experiments; use `CL-##` until formal numbered experiments are allocated safely.

## Relationship to older rules

The Experiment-359 epistemic reset closed unregistered source-*confirmation* and repeated optimization without new evidence. It did not, and should not, eliminate *hypothesis invention*. Earlier semantic quarantines remain in force for claims of evidentiary success. They do not prohibit a clearly labeled exploratory excursion, including into formerly overfitted languages, visual overlays, or 23-based hypotheses, provided earlier exact counterexamples stay visible.
