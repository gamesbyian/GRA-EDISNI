## CL-01 v1: the nine class words are addresses, and Q4 selects a channel

**Hunch:** Pretend each A–I class's nine slash/dash body symbols specify a record address in a large external data table. Pretend its three-symbol Q4 tail specifies one of three channels to inspect at that address. The real product could be a printed directory, source-image atlas, website data table or puzzle-state grid. This hunch is motivated by the historically suggested 9+3 “big number + small selector” representation (Experiment 399), **not** by any verified source providing that reader.

### Assumption stack

1. **OBSERVED:** nine A–I background classes, 9+3 foreground split, slash/dash versus slash/dot zones.
2. **HISTORICALLY ATTESTED:** first nine class cells can be treated as a 9-bit number and final three as a three-state selector. See Experiment 399.
3. **BORROWED ANALOGY:** an ARG can use a clue as an address into an external artifact, as the original background actually addressed a Terminal41 path.
4. **INVENTED BRIDGE:** there is a coherent indexed collection with hundreds of entries and three distinct read channels at each entry.
5. **INVENTED BRIDGE:** reading the nine addressed/channel-selected records in a fixed order yields a subsequent instruction, route, or art layer.

### Hypothetical human walkthrough

A Collector's Edition owner notices each physical A–I class repeating across serial copies; pooled copies yield 12 marks per class. A period or cover clue induces treating first-nine marks as bits, last-three as a one-hot channel. Read each class's nine bits as integer `0..511`, select channel `0/1/2` according to the slash in its final three positions, look up nine records in `A..I` order, and concatenate one field or image fragment from each. The resulting phrase identifies the next game/Terminal41 interaction or explains the CE's damaged page.

**The underlined leap of imagination:** we are *assuming a suitable 512-address × three-channel source exists*. We have not located it. Nine sketches in a poster or 74 archive URLs alone do not constitute that source.

### Exact conditional consequence now tested

The reproducible [CL-01 program](../../scripts/conjecture_lab_nine_bit_address_probe.py) enumerates the existing **18** physically compatible primary-column masters and **18** compatible Q4 depth codes; product = **324** complete H108 masters. For each of four fixed bit readings, it evaluates every one of the nine address outputs across the 18 primary vectors, without selecting a pretty completion.

| Binary reading | Slash bit | Primary vectors fitting a zero-based 74-entry table | Minimum possible largest address | Minimum table length (zero-based) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Forward | 1 | 0/18 | 407 | **408** |
| Forward | 0 | 0/18 | 453 | **454** |
| Reverse | 1 | 0/18 | 473 | **474** |
| Reverse | 0 | 0/18 | 327 | **328** |

In the ordinary forward slash=1 reading the full per-class address support is:

```
A 298 / 302       B 382                 C 58
D 147/151/155/159/211/215/219/223      E 311/319
F 407             G 305/309/313/317/369/373/377/381
H 129             I 357/365
```

Four classes have immutable numeric values (B=382, C=58, F=407, H=129) inside **this** conditional ensemble. The maximal address in every one of the 18 body vectors is 407. Q4 tail supports by class, with depth counted 0/1/2, are `A:{0,1,2}, B:{2}, C:{0,1,2}, D:{1}, E:{0}, F:{1}, G:{0,2}, H:{2}, I:{2}` (18 full codes).

**Result:** direct unmodified nine-address lookup into the specific **74 archived Terminal41 route entries** is impossible in this A∩B family for all four fixed bit directions/polarities. This remains true for all 324 full completions, because Q4 does not change the 9-bit body addresses. A one-based interpretation needs a table numbered at least through 407 in the native scheme. The archive is **not** authenticated as a complete original site catalog, and the 74 routes are not known to be a numerical index; this test rules out only the specified toy reader.

### New hypothetical artifact requirement and prospective discriminator

If CL-01 is close to intended, expect an author-associated indexed source capable of addressing **at least 408 zero-based records under the native bit convention**, and some identifiable *three-channel structure*. If a source with only 74 legitimate addresses is recovered and independently marked as *the* intended reader with direct binary addresses, CL-01's specific non-modular form is dead. If a 512-record three-channel atlas is found, that is a *candidate source*, not proof the stickers refer to it. Freeze any indexing rule and look for an unused prediction on that actual source. The same logic allows a 512-slot texture atlas, a large book, or a coded dataset; it does not justify declaring that any exists.

**Disposition:** keep as a potentially informative but costly hunch; reject the direct 74-entry archive join. High-leverage next step is native-source inventory for a 512-class / three-channel structure, *not* semantic fitting modulo 74 until separately documented as an invented variant.

## CL-02 v1: the tail is a nine-move physical instruction, used only once

**Hunch:** Pretend the 27 dot/slash cells encode **nine ternary commands**, one per A–I class, with the slash's position across three rows being the 0/1/2 command. The 81 primary cells carry a *different* image, verification pattern or checksum. No recursive G5/G6 is required. Treat the known in-game three-way lever as a **design analogy**, not a demonstrated nine-move receiver.

### Hypothetical human walkthrough

Assemble 12 consecutive 3×3 foreground frames and notice that the final three frames change alphabet. For each A–I class, read the height of its single Q4 slash (top/middle/bottom). Arrange these nine selector depths in the poster's solved 3×3 class geometry `IAB/CDE/FGH` and interpret them as commands to nine physical locations or a nine-stage machine. The first 81 marks then supply something else: an image to verify the machine's output, a page-address field, or a three-quarter initial condition.

**Invented bridges:** (i) an authored one-slash-tail instruction, (ii) a three-command label mapping, (iii) a nine-command external reader. The existing original bunker password contains fourteen actions, not nine. Experiment 343/479 closes direct replay of that known password; CL-02 cannot claim otherwise.

### Exact conditional breadth

With current observations and the one-slash assumption, the nine ternary depths admit **18** strings:

```
020101022 020101222 021101022 021101222 022101022 022101222
120101022 120101222 121101022 121101222 122101022 122101222
220101022 220101222 221101022 221101222 222101022 222101222
```

Under all six arbitrary bijections of depths to `L,R,U`, these give **108 distinct nine-command strings**. Allowing reverse direction increases the distinct candidate set to **192**, not simply 216 because several reversed strings duplicate forward ones. Thus this is a compact *finite* conjecture, but no particular nine-command output is independently determined. It predicts that a valid native nine-position three-choice receiver should be identifiable; a direct 14-move password consumer is out.

The already tested **different** Q4 selected-depth readout (Experiment 451) produces **68** possible 27-bit binary outputs across A∩B, or three across the narrower selected-column family. Those outputs contain no dot/Left action. This is not evidence for CL-02; it makes clear why converting *selected body bits* into the three-direction lever is not enough. The present conjecture consumes the **tail depths as commands** rather than the body selections as commands.

### Risky next test

Look for an original CE/game or archived ARG artifact with **nine independently selectable three-state positions** that existed around the CE release. A nine-sketch poster is only nine pictures and has no demonstrated three-way choice per sketch. A legitimate 3×3×3 receiver would make CL-02 unusually concrete. A complete native consumer inventory proving no such action surface exists in the relevant materials would weaken it sharply, though missing server history limits such proof.

**Disposition:** a legitimate alternative human puzzle story, not an established decoder. Hold the depth vocabulary 0/1/2 fixed for future comparison; no retrospectively chosen favorite nine-command string.

## CL-03 v1: the reversible cover is an operation keybook

**Hunch:** Pretend the cover's prior-ARG monitor images are *instructions about what to do* rather than pixel layers to overlay with 108 marks. They could direct: order fragments as in the acorn puzzle, read three-state coordinates from the planet/binary printer, invert a layout because the sleeve is reversible, then submit an output to an existing terminal or print mechanism.

### Hypothetical author-then-player walkthrough

1. The designer puts traces of previously solved printer/planet/terminal steps on the clue-bearing cover. The intended buyer is expected to recognize these operation families.
2. A pooled set of sticker foregrounds gives a body plus an anomalous tail. The **assumed** role of the cover is to label an operation sequence: first reorder or register; second choose from three channels; third feed a short object into the already-identified background URL or a printer-derived receiver.
3. The player derives an exact intermediate artifact (image/instruction/address), performs the implied operation, and receives a result independent of another choice of source/layout. The website may now be defunct; a preserved original page might permit documentary confirmation.

### Counterweight, not immediate execution ban

Source work has found that the acorn seems partial on the cover, but a common geometric transform across other depicted monitors has **not** been independently demonstrated (Experiments 409–412). The cover was also available with standalone PS4 packaging, so a CE-only interpretation needs to explain why that separately distributed clue matters. Poster publicity likewise features the same nine-sketch scene in both CE and standalone imagery (Experiment 481).

**Invented bridges:** the order of operations, exactly which displayed monitor is a key, and the sticker-to-monitor registration. None is confirmed. Simply mirroring/extracting image features that look like letters is not an authored solution.

### Distinct predictions from CL-01/02

This theory predicts **one repeated operation signature across at least two cover panels**, discoverable without reference to the sticker predictions. For instance, two source-image pairs should show the *same measurable crop/rotation/omission* or *same margin-based permutation* if “the cover teaches the transform” is literally true. It also predicts a reproducible choice of ordering across the foreground layout, supplied externally by the cover rather than by optimizing image tidiness. Lack of such repeated instruction patterns would downgrade the specific keybook version.

**Disposition:** high-value visual/source provenance challenge, weak current mechanism; continue by testing the actual panel/source pairs before inventing image coordinates.

## Critical competitor: no additional endgame

A finite direct channel may never have existed for CE foregrounds; it might be an abandoned promotion, a standalone teaser or an intentionally communal incomplete layer. This competing story predicts no recoverable active receiving interface after CE, a manufacturer-controlled serial-cycle rather than puzzle progression, and no further source-native instruction. Present absence of an intact receiver does not by itself establish this story; it is a control against inventing a mandatory finale.

## Why this is different from the earlier research

This sprint did not wait for a documented 512-record reader to *form* CL-01. It assumed one, pursued the consequences, then showed that the most obvious existing 74-route collection does not meet the assumption. CL-02 did not require recursion or a certified interface before deriving the complete menu of possible nine-command strings. CL-03 was developed as an author's instruction-book hypothesis even though literal image overlays previously failed. The evidence bar has not shifted; the willingness to pursue a contingent story has.
