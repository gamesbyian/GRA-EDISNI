# Conjecture Lab CL-02 v4: an unexpected arithmetic progression

_8 October 2026. EXPLORATORY / POST-SELECTION. Companion to [second sprint](2026-10-08-second-sprint.md). Reproducible [exact null calculation](../../scripts/conjecture_lab_progression_null.py)._

The two balance-plus-selected-surface full masters have a **single invariant three-row readout**, as previously documented:

```
-/-////-/ = 189
-//////-- = 252
/--///-// = 315
```

Each row is interpreted as a nine-bit number, slash = 1, read in A-I order. **189, 252, 315 form an exact nonconstant arithmetic progression with step 63.** Under slash=0 the series becomes 322, 259, 196 (step -63). Reverse bit order does not preserve that relation.

This numerical feature was noticed **after** the two model assumptions and output rules were chosen. It is a candidate-generating curiosity, not source-independent evidence.

## Why multiples of seven are inevitable

A selected row has exactly one dash in each physical column of `IAB/CDE/FGH`. Under a native A-I nine-bit number the three columns correspond to bit-exponent sets `{0,3,6}`, `{2,5,8}`, and `{1,4,7}`. Since `2³ = 1 mod 7`, picking a single dash from each set gives total dash weight congruent to `1+4+2 = 0 mod 7`. All-slash `511` is also divisible by seven. Therefore the decoded value `511 - dash-weight` is **always divisible by seven** for every allowed surface, not just this output.

The observed three values are consequently `7×(27,36,45)`. Their quotient progression with step 9 is **not generally forced** by the one-dash-per-column rule, whereas the factor seven is. A supposed “7 clue” generated by this method would mostly be an artifact of the candidate grammar.

## Exact selection-aware controls

- One allowed nine-bit surface: `3³ = 27` possibilities, representing three independent choices of which of three cells receives a dash.
- Three independently chosen surfaces: `27³ = 19,683` ordered triples. Among those, **191** are arithmetic progressions, including **27** constant triples; **164** have nonzero steps; **eight** increase by exactly 63.
- The narrower, *actually selected* Experiment-450 A∩B∩S completion family has **12** full masters and just **three** distinct selected-output triplets. **Four of twelve** masters yield `189,252,315`, and the newly invented global Q4 3/3/3 balance restriction retains **two of those four**.

The 191/19,683 broad control frequency (~0.97%) is **not a statistical confirmation** of our exact readout. The parent rules and balance were chosen after examining the same sticker data, and the selection-conditioned reference is substantially less surprising: 4/12 masters under the preexisting selected-depth grammar.
