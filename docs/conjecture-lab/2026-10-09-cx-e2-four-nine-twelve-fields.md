## The extra deduction

```text

roles:    head4  /       body9      /          tail12
lengths:     4   1          9      1             12
```

The striking arithmetic is **4 + 1 + 9 + 1 + 12 = 27**. The numbers 4, 9 and 12 resemble four historical printer schemes/cover monitors, nine image classes, and twelve observations per class in a 108-site cycle. This is a **post-hoc design analogy**, not three pieces of evidence.

The *real* methodological advantage is a typed domain bridge. Every nine **consecutive serial positions** contains each of A–I **exactly once**, because the background classes repeat with period nine. In each quarter, positions 6–14 enumerate `F G H I A B C D E`, a complete class set. Given the independently solved physical image geometry

```text

```

the nine mark values may be placed on one **image-class 3×3 surface** without a free 9! permutation. This does **not** require mistaking nine occurrences of one class for nine different classes (the domain error ruled out by Experiment 319 for other constructions).

**Limitation:** the class mapping is automatic for **any** nine consecutive residues, not unique to positions 6–14. What the observed rails potentially supply is the *choice of window*. A source must still show that the slash bars mean delimiters.
