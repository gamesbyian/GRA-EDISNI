# Audit: hypothesis permission, hunches and abductive research in the INSIDE sticker project

_Date: 8 October 2026. Status: methodology audit and proposed research policy, not an additional decoded result._

## Executive judgment

**There is a real imbalance, but it is more precise than “the project never speculates.”** The project has generated many speculative families, from one-hot POS3 and recursive selectors to 23-based endpoints, cube transfers, Pigpen, the cover, and the damaged 534brn page. It has also built exceptionally good safeguards against false positives. The imbalance lies in **permission to develop an unsupported hypothesis far enough to find what it would predict**. Current instructions frequently require a pre-existing independently licensed operation *before* exploring a possible correspondence. That is a good threshold for a claimed discovery, not necessarily a good threshold for the *creation* of one.

**Policy recommendation:** preserve the entire observation/verification discipline, but create a first-class conjecture lane in which agents may say: “There is no sufficient evidence for A; assume A provisionally and investigate B, C and D.” No speculative result may silently enter the canonical observation ledger or machine specification. A conjecture may nevertheless be explored, mutated, backsolved, or pursued across several unsupported bridges before its evidentiary status changes.

A useful maxim: **unsupported is a research status, not an automatic stopping rule.** Contradicted by a frozen physical observation is a substantially different status.

Detailed close reading concentrated on the controlling documents and representative experiments: 317–340, 359–367, 378–395, 407–433, 448–459, 465–466, and 476–481, together with destination, archival, source, human-solve, and hypothesis-map reports.

- This document audits *research behavior*. It does not alter the physical H108 observations (84 sticker records, 66 unique residues, 42 unseen residues), the 427-dot falsification of the frozen E2 pair, or the conditional status of POS3, G5/G6, and terminal 100.
- Existing closed negative results remain exactly as bounded as their original test. No rule here reclassifies them as positive evidence.

### Findings by severity

**A. High: epistemic gate applied at the wrong stage.** `AGENTS.md` requires stating the bounded parent operation before inspecting attractive outputs; `docs/sticker-epistemic-reset.md` R3 says “Implement only historically demonstrated operation classes”; its closing paragraph allows reopening primarily on independently supplied cues. `docs/research-queue.md` discourages unconstrained interpretation. These are appropriate **verification** restrictions, but they also constrain abduction, cross-domain analogy and abductive story-building during **generation**. A hypothesis cannot always identify its final consumer in advance.

**B. High: stopping is too often binary.** The queue and evidence-map method correctly distinguish fixed failures from unregistered jumps, yet “obtain authorial parameter or stop” (concept-map crosslink, Finding E) treats *absence of a recovered historical parameter* as a reason not to pursue a hypothetical combination. The correct disposition should be one of: falsified, expensive, low-priority, conjecture-lab eligible, or waiting for a source. Only the first is a true exclusion of a frozen hypothesis. A speculative branch may be parked for budget reasons, but it should not become conceptually forbidden.

**C. High: too few fully worked speculative chains.** The destination investigation is a welcome exception: it lists nine possible endgames (damaged JPEG, cover composite, mission/status, three-way input, index cipher, generated artifact, audiovisual teaser, Xbox residual number layer, standalone/decommissioned puzzle). Most entries catalogue missing links and stop gates. Their next missing step is often treated as a reason to search only for documentation, rather than an invitation to temporarily *supply* that step, work forward, and discover what else becomes necessary or unexpectedly matches.

**D. Medium: source-assisted recognition is underused as a discovery strategy.** Experiments 465–466 explain that the solved A–I background route was apparently recognized after partial reading plus site-index discovery. They correctly deny it the status of a blind full decode. But that is a positive precedent for **candidate retrieval and backsolving**. With careful disclosure of the selection process and a final independent discriminator, using a finite source dictionary to inspire a decoding hypothesis is scientifically legitimate *exploration*. Forbidding target-assisted inspiration entirely would exclude a method that assisted an actual historical solution.

**E. Medium: model refinement has more infrastructure than model invention.** The thematic title screen shows sustained formal attention to selectors/route/gauge/completion and extensive negative-control practice. This makes valid conclusions more reliable but steers the next agent toward readily executable refinements rather than high-risk conceptual leaps. The 37-card evidence matrix answers “what is supported or testable?”; it lacks an analogous catalog answering “which surprising counterfactual worlds have been imaginatively developed?” Treat a conjecture portfolio as a **different artifact**, not a competing evidence scorecard.

**F. Medium: some semantic searches have been banned rather than demoted.** The 14,400 possible nine-letter Pigpen strings in Experiment 335 correctly make an English-looking result worthless as *evidence*. They do not make a limited dictionary/phrase search worthless as *hypothesis generation* if every searched output, parameter, and comparison is disclosed and no discovered hit is declared probative until it makes a genuinely novel prediction. Similar distinctions apply to XOR imagery, 23/Hollerith numerology, 621 endpoints, alternate 3D orientations, and terminal-100 analogies.

**G. Medium: incomplete negative results may acquire folk-universality.** Examples: Experiment 476 excludes **literal unchanged Xbox rows**, not a printer-derived reordering or overlays; Experiment 479 excludes direct reuse of one known lever password under the tested orderings, not every three-direction interface; Experiment 423 cannot infer a unique MCU count from damaged entropy, but the historical `128 UNSOLVED` clue is still source-native; cube-copy failures do not prohibit cube-as-instruction. The docs usually qualify these accurately, but the agent-level quarantines can obscure the distinctions in future exploration.

**H. Strength worth protecting:** the repo *does* demonstrate successful conjecture development and falsification. The recursive machine was developed conditionally before its use was proven. The row-one-exception family, Q4 depth vs quarter rivals and the frozen `534brn` E2 pair show that hunches can turn into crisp physical forecasts; E2's contradiction from sticker 427 is a real success of the research process. Do not discard that mechanism of intellectual accountability.

## Working distinction: three legitimate modes

1. **DISCOVER (free exploration):** Form hunches, use analogy, look at outputs, perform target-assisted searches, choose temporary parameter values, reconstruct a plausible authorial story, and chase surprises. Put `EXPLORATORY / SELECTED AFTER INSPECTION` on results. No p-values, uniqueness claims, likelihood boosts or physical sticker “discoveries” from this mode.
2. **DEVELOP (conditional consequence):** Hold assumptions fixed for a sustained period: “Pretend A is true.” Deduce the required representation, readout, external artifact, impossible conditions, costs and novel consequences. Compare entire coherent completion ensembles, rival assumptions and negative controls where practical. If a branch changes its hypothesis, version it rather than quietly rewriting it.
3. **VALIDATE (evidence and closure):** Freeze the chosen operation and parameters before new evidence, use held-out/new physical observations or independently checkable artifact outputs, and distinguish original source authority from answer-assisted recognition. Existing strict rules, dependency tracking, guards against false positives and reproducible acceptance all apply here.

**Asymmetry rule:** The bar for **exploring** a hypothesis is only that it is expressible, coherent enough to reason about, and sufficiently interesting per effort. The bar for **claiming** it remains unchanged.

**No easy loophole:** Exploratory backsolving can generate excellent leads, but no amount of post hoc fit on the same H108 cells establishes that Playdead intended it. An effective conjecture should eventually expose at least one risky consequence *not used to invent it*.

## Proposed conjecture-card format

Every active “pretend this is true” project gets a versioned card:

- **Hunch statement:** a one-sentence conjecture with an explicit provisional commitment (“For this experiment assume…”).
- **Assumption stack:** 1–4 bridges with types: observed, historical, borrowed analogy, invented parameter, or physically contradicted. Never silently merge them.
- **Why it might have been authored:** intuitive human puzzle move, thematic/lore fit, product chronology and real player information state. This is a generative rationale, not independent support.
- **If true, then…:** enumerate concrete outputs, exact geometry/transform(s), side effects, artifacts which *should* exist, and surprise-producing implications.
- **Exploratory permission:** which fitting, semantic inspection, rotations, completions or target-assisted dictionary search is deliberately permitted.
- **Failure and survival map:** distinguish contradiction of the frozen branch, failure to find a consumer, failure of an assumed source condition, and weak explanatory power.
- **Next risky discriminator:** one previously unused datum, external native behavior, physical mark or out-of-sample artifact prediction that could kill the branch.
- **Stop rule:** budget/novelty and remaining information gain. Lack of independent proof **alone** does not close exploration.
- **Outcome:** a candidate chain, new experimentally grounded prediction, conditional impossibility, useful negative, or abandoned conjecture. State which.

A provisional assumption is neither a raw sticker fact nor a license to publish an invented plaintext as the answer.

## Proposed first research sprint

Do not begin by calculating more uniqueness theorems about terminal 100. Run **three competing conjecture tracks in parallel**, with independent premises and independent work products:

- **Track A: destination-first answer-assisted backsolve.** Draft and execute two complete hypothetical sticker→534brn chains; compare against the 74-route background dictionary as *exploration*, keep exact parameter provenance, and extract two new predictions for some *other* source/physical datum.
- **Track B: Q4 instruction-first.** Freeze three human-readable one-shot semantics (row select, physical depth select, per-class command) on the same 84-record corpus and propagate U2, depth and quarter ensembles. Deliberately test candidates even without a pre-existing external reader; score subsequent validation separately.
- **Track C: cover/physical-prop author walkthrough.** Starting from what a 2019 purchaser physically handled, imagine three complete end-to-end solution scenarios, write their required moves in order, and check the fixed artifacts for omitted instructions or impossibilities. Distinguish CE-only from separately available poster/cover content.

Have a **fourth adversarial pass** attempt to falsify the most appealing candidate and calculate its selection freedom. Do not make adversarial review a prerequisite to drafting the first imaginative version. Preserve the same physical observations, source history and correction logs across tracks.

Suggested initial allocation, explicitly a management experiment rather than an evidence-derived optimum: 25% discovery, 35% conditional development, 40% verification/source acquisition. Review after real results. Every cycle should produce at least one **worked conditional solution narrative** and at least one **risky predicted observation**, rather than only a list of unavailable cues.

## Proposed change to repository agent rules

Add the following under a new “Conjecture research mode” heading in `AGENTS.md` when adopting this proposal:

> Hypothesis generation is expressly allowed to be bold. An agent may provisionally assume an unproved decoding rule, source alignment, destination, manufacturing endpoint, or authorial intention and follow its implications across multiple steps, even when no independent clue yet supplies that operation. Label assumptions and selected-after-inspection outputs unambiguously; store such work as exploratory conjectures, never in the raw-observation register or as validated machine conclusions. Existing negative results rule out only their fixed tested implementations. Advance a conjecture by deriving a novel risky consequence, reducing its unsupported assumption stack, or producing an exact conditional impossibility. Require independently checked evidence only **when promoting** a result to a puzzle finding, not when allowing it to be investigated.

Change the `R3` text in the epistemic reset from “implement only historically demonstrated operation classes” to **“for confirmatory operation replay, restrict to historically demonstrated or source-fixed classes; a separately labeled conjecture lane may deliberately invent and explore other operations.”** Interpret the Experiment-359 stopping boundary as **closure for unregistered evidence claims and repetitive null sweeps, not closure for imaginative conditional model construction**.

The evidence map remains a map of evaluative studies. Add a *separate* conjecture portfolio with fields for assumption stack, developed pathway, potential output, unexamined dependencies, failure mode and discriminator. Its purpose is exploration, **not another score for theory truth**.

## Methodological rationale

- C. S. Peirce's abduction–deduction–induction cycle explicitly treats making a surprising explanatory guess as a phase before deducing its consequences and confronting evidence: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/peirce/
- T. C. Chamberlin, *The Method of Multiple Working Hypotheses* (1890; 1897 printing), argues for several provisional interpretations instead of premature attachment to a ruling theory: https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/607980
- J. W. Tukey, *We Need Both Exploratory and Confirmatory* (1980), distinguishes exploration that generates ideas from later tightly planned confirmation: https://doi.org/10.1080/00031305.1980.10482706
- Modern exploratory/pre-registration guidance preserves validity precisely by **labeling** the exploratory/confirmatory divide, not by banning exploratory data-dependent thinking: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7725296/
- The project already uses complementary evidence maps and constraint validation. None of the above supports assigning false probabilities or declaring an explored coincidence an answer.

## Bottom line

The existing machine, observations, exact null tests, evidence maps and negative results are assets. Keep them intact. The missing tool is *permission to hold an unjustified premise provisionally for long enough to see where it leads*, with a clear firewall between an imaginative idea and an evidential claim. The project should be both a disciplined laboratory and a place where hunches are permitted to grow into testable theories.
