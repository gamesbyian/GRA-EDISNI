# INSIDE CE sticker: decoding-operation atlas (12 October 2026)

**Mode: DISCOVER / DEVELOP.** Companion to the [82 receiving roles](receiving-surface-atlas-2026-10-12.md) and [bridge-test program](closed-corpus-reader-bridge-program-2026-10-12.md). This is a **long inventory of distinguishable operations**, not 144 equally likely mechanisms. **No plaintext, completion, instruction or CE foreground endpoint is verified.**

**Physical baseline:** 84 stickers, 66 unique H108 residues, 42 missing, image classes A–I, repeated serial phase, apparent 81 slash/dash plus 27 slash/dot zoning, solved A–I physical layout \`IAB/CDE/FGH\`. U2=324, U5=10 are **conditional models**, not unconstrained truths. Treat unconfirmed claims of mid-body dots separately.

**Source shorthand:** **S** = source/native physical sticker fact, **H** = historically demonstrated INSIDE ARG operation or contemporaneous idea, **M** = existing model's *conditional* consequence, **E** = deliberately invented exploratory bridge. These tags describe provenance, **not independent confirmation**. Tier 1 requires a small fixed corpus/source check; Tier 2 needs a new source-anchored parameter/consumer; Tier 3 requires substantial assumption invention. **F** flags a narrow implementation already negatively tested, not an entire hypothesis family.

Core sources: [mechanics corpus](arg-puzzle-mechanics-corpus.md), [sticker reset](sticker-epistemic-reset.md), [36 speculative revivals](speculative-revival-portfolio-2026-10-09.md), [model map](experiment-concept-map.md), [experiment index](experiment-title-index.md), [completion universe](experiment-365-completion-universe.md), [current status](current-state.md), [receiver history](experiment-476-receiver-native-geometry-audit.md), [destination inventory](sticker-endgame-destination-investigation-2026-10-08.md).

## Audited negatives and non-independence

- **Direct Xbox row reuse** (D006), direct known 14-move bunker replay (D102), straight limited parser grammars (D103), the one-dot tail family (D046), direct 74-route nine-bit lookup (D095), and exact six gate98-key RGB packing (D115) have specific bounded failures. Each needs a *new independently specified feature* before re-testing.
- **A model that fits does not license its own decoder.** POS3, selected-depth, recursion, terminal 100 and fixed-point theory are dependent conditional interpretations. The source-native clue to *perform the operation* remains unresolved.
- **The same 66 observations are reused throughout.** Do not score one family by counting how many correlated tests it passed. Invariance across U2 means all 324 conditional completions share a result, **not** that the real intended code has been uniquely decoded.
- **Status chronology matters.** The October 2026 PRs on Steam, RGB, variable-length instructions, 128-character output, lever rotations and CP-SAT contain exploratory/negative experiments; an unmerged PR is not a finding already promoted to main. See current PRs #181, #184–188.
- **There is no proof the stickers are ECC.** The repeated physical 108-cell cycle is replication. Linear/RS/BCH proposals require a source-fixed codebook, field, framing or strong independent out-of-sample parity signature.
