# Experiment 353 — G5 independent-consumer evidence audit

_Status: completed bounded reset audit, 1 Oct 2026._

## Question

After Experiment 352, the primary 3×3 body has an independently supported one-minority-per-column skeleton and a lossless three-position coordinate per column. The highest-priority remaining circularity target is G5:

> use the tail selector as an address/depth substitution on the body.

This experiment asks the narrower pre-machine question:

> Does the reset-era historical or cross-puzzle evidence independently specify **that operation class**, rather than merely a body+selector architecture?

No incumbent-machine output is used to score evidence.

## Frozen evidence set

This audit is limited to the already-canonical reset sources:

- `docs/historical-ordering-acorn-lineage.md`;
- `docs/arg-puzzle-mechanics-corpus.md`;
- Experiment 317's observation-only one-of-three tail compatibility;
- Experiments 348–352's historical/frame/coordinate results.

The admissible support levels are:

1. **exact operation:** tail/index explicitly replaces or selects a body coordinate/address;
2. **operation class:** selector/index is explicitly applied to a structured body, but the selected axis is unspecified;
3. **architecture only:** body+selector/index is proposed, with no operation tying the selector to a body coordinate;
4. **generic precedent:** another INSIDE puzzle uses an earlier output as a selector, without sticker-specific registration.

## Result

No source in the frozen set reaches level 1.

The May-2026 historical 9+3 proposal reaches **level 2 only in the broad sense**: the first nine symbols encode a larger domain and the final three encode a second number selecting within the first result. This independently motivates a layered body+index consumer, but does not identify physical depth, q/d/j coordinates, coordinate replacement, recursion, or a second selector application.

The solved-ARG mechanics corpus contributes level 4: Playdead demonstrably reused earlier outputs as selectors when the earlier stage supplied exact parameters. That raises the prior for selector consumption but supplies no sticker-specific axis or substitution rule.

Experiment 317 independently supports a one-of-three tail code, and Experiments 348–352 independently support a three-coordinate body representation. Their coexistence makes selector-on-body families cheap to test, but does not by itself establish G5. Treating matching ternary cardinalities as an instruction would reintroduce the circularity the reset is designed to remove.

## Consequence for G5

G5 remains a substantive model-level hypothesis with **medium-high circularity risk**.

The reset has, however, narrowed its unsupported burden. The evidence now independently supplies:

- a 3×3 body with three positional coordinates;
- a one-of-three tail/index family;
- historical body+index architecture;
- a Playdead precedent for externally supplied selectors.

What remains unsupported is the bridge:

> **which body coordinate is selected/replaced, and why?**

Therefore the next admissible G5 test must discriminate a small set of equally simple selector-on-body consumers without using incumbent route/terminal success to choose among them. Candidate consumers must be specified from physical geometry or historical wording before output inspection.

Do not promote `(q,d,j)->(q,S(j),j)` merely because it preserves the incumbent machine.

## Closure rule

This audit closes the search for an already-present exact historical G5 cue in the canonical reset synthesis. Reopen historical provenance only if a newly recovered primary message, diagram, packaging feature, or external artifact explicitly identifies the selected body axis or substitution operation.
