# Experiment 357 — G7 provenance boundary audit

_Status: completed bounded archival/reset audit, 1 Oct 2026._

## Question

Experiment 321 weakens G7 from route semantics to two generic information-preservation priors:

1. each selected ternary word preserves all three ternary labels;
2. the three functional families collectively use all three q indices exactly once.

Their intersection uniquely selects q choices `(2,1,0)` and words `120/012/102`.

Experiment 357 asks whether either of those selection rules is independently present in the preserved pre-machine sticker-solving record.

## Frozen archive

Primary source:

- especially `Playdead Unofficial - ARG - solving-breakout [463106924708233216].txt`

Searches covered route/routing, reversible, permutation, one-of-each, all-three, use-each, distinct, three-output, and related wording.

## Result

No recovered sticker-solving message independently proposes any equivalent of:

- choose one q-indexed output from each of three functional families;
- require each chosen three-symbol word to contain all three ternary labels;
- require the three families to use q positions 0,1,2 exactly once;
- select three reversible ternary maps;
- derive q=2-p;
- expect a three-route shell.

The archive does contain unrelated permutation language, chiefly:

- De Bruijn / superpermutation discussion for brute-forcing bunker lever codes;
- image-row or 3×3 block shuffling for speculative sticker reconstructions;
- Sudoku-style one-of-each observations.

Those are not operation-level precedents for G7 and should not be counted as support.

## Internal evidence

G7 nevertheless has a small exact search space.

From the three first-pass functional families, there are only `3^3 = 27` ways to choose one q-indexed word from each family.

Experiment 321 shows:

- 4 assignments preserve all ternary labels in every selected word;
- 6 assignments use all three q positions exactly once;
- exactly 1 assignment satisfies both:
  `(2,1,0) -> 120/012/102`.

No route semantics, mutual-distinctness target, terminal `100`, or expected q formula is required.

## Epistemic result

G7 is **not historically motivated** by the currently preserved sticker-solving corpus.

Its best status remains:

> a generic information-preservation / non-collapse prior whose bounded finite consequence is unique.

This is materially weaker than an externally supplied clue, but materially stronger than arbitrary route fitting.

## Consequence

Do not search broader route semantics merely because `120/012/102` looks meaningful. The relevant finite selection family is already exhausted.

Reopen G7 only if new evidence independently suggests:

- reversible ternary mappings;
- one-of-each coordinate use;
- conservation of all three labels;
- or an external artifact containing a registered three-route structure.

Absent such evidence, G7 should remain explicitly conditional and downstream route language should be treated as interpretation, not proof.
