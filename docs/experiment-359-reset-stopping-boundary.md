# Experiment 359 — reset stopping-boundary audit

_Status: completed epistemic-reset closeout audit, 1 Oct 2026._

## Question

After Experiments 317–358, does the closed-corpus epistemic-reset program still have a high-value reason to keep widening hypothesis families, or has it reached a defensible stopping boundary?

This experiment does not select a final sticker solution. It audits the reset's own exit criteria and separates:

1. work that is complete enough to stop repeating;
2. live conditional assumptions that should remain explicit;
3. genuinely discriminating evidence that would justify reopening a lane.

No new machine family, plaintext criterion, route target, or hidden-cell prediction is introduced.

## Frozen criteria

The audit uses the six exit criteria in `docs/sticker-epistemic-reset.md`:

1. historical solving record mined into reproducible operations;
2. solved neighboring ARG mechanics catalogued at operation level;
3. incumbent premises audited against that corpus;
4. plausible simpler families given cheap falsification attempts;
5. surviving families compared on independent evidence and prospective prediction;
6. research queue states clearly which assumptions remain live.

## Criterion audit

### 1. Historical operations — sufficiently complete for closed-corpus work

Completed work includes:

- historical ordering/acorn-lineage reconstruction;
- exact historical 12-row permutation replay;
- historical twelve-square census reconstruction;
- chronological prequential replay of the 3/6 versus 4/5 census;
- historical three-position / ternary / body+index provenance;
- lever-consumer lineage and its direct-order falsification.

Remaining historical gaps are refinements, not unbounded search licenses: exact missing message anchors, unarchived attachments, or a newly recovered statement that explicitly supplies a selector, ordering, reuse, or registration rule.

### 2. Neighboring ARG operation corpus — sufficiently complete for discrimination

The operation-level corpus now distinguishes operations that are:

- directly licensed now;
- pending a specific cue;
- closed until a cue appears;
- already tested negatively.

The reset therefore no longer needs generic “what would Playdead do?” expansion. New neighboring-puzzle material matters only if it supplies an operation class or parameter not already represented.

### 3. Incumbent premise audit — complete at the supplied-premise level

O1/O2/G1/G3/G5/G6/G7 have been classified and attacked.

Key reset-era changes:

- G1's physical 3/6 one-per-column skeleton gains independent chronological support;
- G3's one-of-three tail/index architecture is independently compatible and historically motivated;
- G5 is strongly constrained inside natural information-preserving families, but the decision to use that consumer remains a model-level operation prior;
- G6 remains unsupported at the decision-to-reuse level, though highly constrained once a second selector-conditioned read is admitted;
- G7 remains a generic information-preservation prior, not historically supplied.

The unresolved items are therefore operation-family choices, not hidden parameter sweeps.

### 4. Simpler-family falsification — sufficiently broad to stop blind widening

Cheap tests have covered, among other lanes:

- tail-only selector families;
- row/chunk versus column/rail interpretations;
- background-derived fixed masks;
- literal-stroke and binary-orbit Pigpen families;
- bounded conventional Pigpen mapping;
- semantic-search-space hazard;
- 9×12 and historical 12×9 row permutation;
- physical perimeter/check-bit observability and a preregistered grayscale perimeter feature;
- direct background-coordinate registration;
- direct known lever-word replay.

Several rivals remain logically possible, but none currently has an independently supplied missing parameter that licenses deeper search.

### 5. Cross-family independent comparison — adequate but not final

> **Audit note (Experiment 365):** the Q4 holdout comparison listed first below is not independent comparative evidence (full-corpus premises are fixed; see Experiment 365). Criterion 5 now rests on the frozen prediction matrix and untested prospective discriminators. Likewise "direct background-coordinate registration" (Exp 333) is falsified only for the row-selector carrier, and the lever replay (Exp 343) assumes the alphabet split extends to unseen cells.

The project has:

- Q4 holdout comparison between the simple one-slash tail family and the incumbent;
- prospective unseen-residue discriminators, especially residues 84 and 102 for the frozen row rival;
- a frozen prediction matrix for new physical evidence;
- explicit acquisition roles for residues that discriminate gauges and hidden states.

This is enough to preserve empirical accountability. It is not enough to declare the incumbent uniquely intended.

### 6. Queue clarity — satisfied after this experiment

The queue should now distinguish three states:

- **closed-corpus complete**: do not widen without new evidence;
- **conditional live**: retain as an explicit assumption, not a fact;
- **reopen trigger**: a concrete new observation or historical cue that discriminates a live question.

## Stopping boundary

The closed-corpus reset has reached a defensible stopping boundary.

Further unconstrained widening is now more likely to increase the number of compatible stories than to increase information. In particular, do not continue by:

- inventing broader route families;
- searching plaintext over underdetermined Pigpen registrations;
- sweeping arbitrary body-to-background masks;
- trying generic image transforms without a sticker-native cue;
- expanding selector maps already exhausted by finite audits;
- treating terminal `100`, route words, or hidden states as external evidence for themselves.

This is a stopping rule for **closed-corpus hypothesis manufacture**, not a declaration that the sticker mystery is solved.

## What remains live

- H108 periodic foreground structure;
- 81 slash/dash + 27 slash/dot alphabet boundary;
- solved A–I background carrier and physical layout;
- 3/6 one-per-column primary occupancy as independently favored by chronological evidence;
- one-of-three tail/index architecture as an independently live family.

### Conditional operation choices

- POS3 as the intended semantic readout of the three-position coordinate;
- G5 as the intended first selector-on-body consumer;
- G6's decision to reuse the selector a second time;
- G7's non-collapse selection rules;
- downstream route, terminal, hidden-state, and request/grant interpretations.

These may remain useful model components, but should be labeled as conditional when presented.

## Reopen triggers

A closed lane may be reopened only by evidence that supplies a missing operation or discriminates a frozen prediction, for example:

1. a new physical sticker at a preregistered discriminator residue;
2. a newly recovered historical message/attachment explicitly specifying ordering, registration, repeated selection, recursion, or a body/index operation;
3. a sticker-native side channel measured independently from foreground predictions;
4. an external ARG artifact that provides a registered selector/key/transform;
5. an independently motivated consumer of the first-pass three-surface object that can be preregistered before inspecting incumbent route/terminal outputs.

New evidence should be entered as observation first, then tested against frozen families before any model repair.

## Operational consequence

The default next move is no longer “design Experiment 360.”

The default is:

- acquisition/mining for genuinely new evidence;
- prospective validation against frozen predictions;
- documentation/provenance repair;
- reproducibility hardening;
- or a clearly cued new hypothesis family.

If none of those is available, stopping is the correct research action.
