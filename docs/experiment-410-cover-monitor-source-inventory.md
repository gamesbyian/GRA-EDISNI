## Structural interpretation

This inventory does not look like a nine-cell keypad, a 9+3 numeric table, or an answer-entry interface.

It looks much more like a **meta-reference composition**:

- known ARG/image families are echoed on monitors;
- at least one is incomplete or transformed;
- some relevant visual information exists outside the monitor plane;
- exact source-game assets appear to be part of the clue vocabulary.

That is an operation-level clue.

## Consequence for sticker work

The cover may legitimately supply a future **registration operation** if a specific monitor/reference pair has a measurable omission, crop, layer, or transform.

But merely counting monitors or assigning sticker values to them is unsupported.

The high-value next question is:

> what exact transformation takes each canonical source image to its cover-screen representation, and is any transformation repeated across multiple monitor/source pairs?

A repeated transform would be a genuine authorial operation cue.

A one-off visual resemblance would not.
