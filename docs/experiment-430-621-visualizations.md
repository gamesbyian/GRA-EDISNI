## What appears


So the large-scale striping is not a newly discovered image. It is the known H108 carrier rendered at its natural 27-cell width.

The observed-only image has a conspicuous blank band at rows 19–22. That corresponds simply to the current lack of observed physical serials 479–594. Row 23 contains only serial 597. This is acquisition-history structure and must not be interpreted as image content.

## Result

The visualizations support the **geometry** of 23×27 if 621 is independently established, but they do not reveal a new image.

Most importantly, they expose a visual trap: any apparent four-row texture in a 23×27 render is inherited directly from H108 and should not be promoted as evidence.
