# INSIDE ARG: historical chronology of puzzles, codes, discoveries and payoffs

*Research cut-off: 12 October 2026. Primary source: [twinysam/INSIDE-ARG](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG), branch `master`, inspected tree `a6cab4c2b3ddb5ba0f801445d324c7f98cc7967f`. Companion evidence: the original public Discord exports, Game Detectives, and source-critical reconstructions already in GRA-EDISNI.*

> **The central distinction:** The Collector's Edition (CE) **nine-piece faint-image** puzzle was solved in April 2020 and led to a real Terminal41 path. The **serial-numbered dot/dash/slash foreground** is an entirely different layer; as of this audit, it has no authenticated final decoder or downstream response. Likewise, an accepted password can precede discovery of its intended derivation, as happened with `LIFEDETECTED`.

## Scope, audit coverage and chronology method

This is a **discovery-and-payoff history**, not an ARG fiction chronology. Publication, first observation, first intelligible partial reconstruction, password acceptance and subsequent explanation can be years apart. The documentation distinguishes them. Dates embedded in fictional breach logs, image metadata, directory indexes or server Last-Modified fields are not automatically dates when a player solved anything.

The source repository's **complete 269-entry tree** was enumerated, including **four top-level Markdown texts**, **69 saved HTML terminal pages**, **four TXT status snapshots**, and **173 original, edited, resized, or other image assets**. All directory families are inventoried below. Its substantial **~1.99 MB `saf_dat_col.html`** and the high-resolution image/binary files are included in the audit by path, provenance and previously completed forensic studies; **this chronology is not a claim to have manually inspected every pixel or decoded every byte**. The text source, 2020 sticker chronology, 82-item photo register, Terminal41 endpoint variants, redirect/status families and secondary historical evidence were cross-compared. The surrounding Game Detectives wiki and historical Discord export help cover the **2016–2019 puzzles omitted from twinysam's shorter README**.

**Evidence categories:** **Confirmed solve** = independently readable output or historically recorded successful receiver response. **Routing payoff** = a meaningful next page/clue is authenticated, even if downstream remains open. **Reported** = community account whose exact original response may not survive. **Structural finding** = an exact carrier/format constraint without plaintext. **Speculation** = proposed operation, not a demonstrated authored solve. **Open** = no documented terminal interpretation or final consumer.

## 2016: the game's initial carriers

**29 June 2016: INSIDE launches and the printer emits the first code family.** At the 12th-last checkpoint, standing in front of a background printer prompts it to print predetermined symbol lines. The PC/PS4 original pool has **41** lines, **32** of length **32** and **nine** shorter, mixing dots, dashes and slashes. Internal assets call the sequences “Morse,” but an ordinary direct Morse transcription never explains them. Their later significance comes from spatial placement, not a flat text stream. See [Game Detectives / PC-PS4](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#PC/PS4) and preserved [reference row files](../data/printer-reference/README.md).

**2016: hidden orbs, the bunker and secret ending.** The game contains a sequence of hidden orb/sphere operations and a three-position lever controlling its secret ending. The existence of a working in-game puzzle consumer matters because, in **2020–21**, community members independently propose that the later sticker marks might direct this very lever. This does **not** establish such a mapping; the ordinary bunker secret was already known. [Game Detectives](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG) and [historical external consumer discussion](discord-historical-technique-inventory.md#sticker-specific-external-consumer-candidate-sleeve-geometry---lever-commands).

**2016: numbered symbols and E. E. Cummings.** Players identify in-world alphanumeric inscriptions and a literary reference tied to E. E. Cummings' *pity this busy monster, manunkind*. Polybius-style interpretation and other numeric/literary reading appear in early community histories. The **literary source itself** later becomes the successful plaintext-selection substrate of the June 2020 macOS printer cipher. The exact first discovery dates for each small 2016 clue are not established by twinysam's repository, so this history deliberately does not fabricate day-level provenance. Remaining inscriptions such as `A10N7`, `L08` and `B02` have no authenticated unified decoder; compare [the 2026 controlled Wow!-signal proposal audit](conjecture-lab/2026-10-09-wow-signal-environmental-inscriptions.md).

## 2017–2018: Terminal41 is staged, printers become decipherable

**1–7 December 2017: Terminal41 server artifacts exist.** Historical HTTP modification evidence retained by Game Detectives dates the base site and `loadSys` redirect sequence to **1 Dec 2017**, and `comms_main_viewgate.html` to **7 Dec 2017**. A multi-page auto-redirect simulates an “emergency terminal link [41]” boot; initial `comms_main_viewgate` says the connection failed and presents facility “89B” diagrams. This is **server-content provenance**, not proof the community found the site those exact days. [Game Detectives Terminal41 dates](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Terminal41), [archived root](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/index.html), [initial viewgate](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/comms_main_viewgate.html).

**4 June 2018: a Playdead website print button is noticed.** Pressing it inserts material on the printed page not present in the ordinary webpage. An absent input produces “no message received”; an unaccepted input, “incorrect message received.” This supplies a concrete testable **password consumer**. Much later GRA forensics locate `/print/prepare.php` and `/print/index.php` as historical POST targets and an HTML response inserted into `#print-content` before `window.print()`; a user-facing PDF does not prove the server returned a PDF body. [Game Detectives printer button](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Printer_Button); [GRA original transport](experiment-480-original-print-transport.md).

**June 2018: the iOS hour-coded branch.** Twenty-four platform-specific **25-cell** monochrome strings each occur at a specified **local hour of day**. Interpreting a 25-cell output as a 5×5 letter and ordering by hour yields **`MULTIPLEPROBESDISPATCHED`**. The Playdead printer-button endpoint **accepts** the phrase and yields an elaborate printed image: a fully solved carrier-to-word-to-receiver chain. Exact first solve day is not pinned in the source consulted; it occurs in the June/July campaign. [Game Detectives iOS](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#iOS).

**June 2018: the Xbox One planet and Braille branch.** Forty-seven unique Xbox printer strings include **35** length-36 rows and a twice-seen row. Rearrangement into a 36×36 round/bell object explains the duplication as matching top/bottom boundaries. Rotate the figure **90° clockwise** and read the raised-dot Braille overlay. The mostly noisy characters contain letter sites that spell **`NEWPLANETDISCOVERED`** (including a historically ambiguous exclamation/S), which is accepted at Playdead's webpage. Its decisive operation is **geometry before decoding**. On **30 June 2018**, archived Discord solvers discuss the bell shape as an ordering constraint. [Game Detectives Xbox](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Xbox_One); [historical ordering](historical-ordering-acorn-lineage.md).

**24–25 June 2018: Terminal41 changes.** The initial `loadSys/__.html` and `loadSys/___________DONE.html` acquire unusual “HIDDE...” punctuation, unlocking awareness of `/sys/` and `/dat/`. On **25 June**, the final boot page changes to `comms_main_viewgate_002.html`: a **successful** connection drawing and reference to `/dat/breachlog`. The gateway status contains a blank input channel, but no saved successful original answer to that form is established. The site changed; the **input that caused it is unverified**. [Game Detectives historical 2018 file-date table](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Terminal41), [second viewgate](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/comms_main_viewgate_002.html).

**25 June–July 2018: the `breachlog` and `printreqstatus` states.** These pages reveal different “schem” positions, security percentages and missing credentials. Source-modification sequence: `printreqstatus` **25 June**, `_002` **27 June**, `_003` **28 June**, `_004/005/006` **1 July**, `_007` **3 July**; more versions through `_016` and `_SD` survive without equally secure first-discovery dates. The saved log includes *fictional* entries dating back to **29 June 2016** and a staged “1 July 2018 breach success” line. Fictional log dates cannot certify individual user actions. [Saved breachlog](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/dat/breachlog.html), [status snapshots](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/tree/master/terminal41.link/sys), [Game Detectives caution](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Terminal41).

**28 June 2018: Switch introduces an instruction code and hardware operation.** Its printer emits binary marks that *can* be read as Morse to give **`CTRL CON DISCON`**, a prompt to connect/disconnect controllers. Players perform **`RRLRLL`** (right/right/left/right/left/left), receiving white-page acknowledgments and eventually a colour-dependent output like `R2G2B6`. Six contemporary Joy-Con colour codes are documented (blue 226, red 311, grey 333, pink 515, green 141, yellow 330 in R/G/B-digit notation). All six status boxes subsequently appear marked, but the wiki explicitly cannot prove which webpage action caused which state. **Operational decoder confirmed; website transition attribution conditional.** [Game Detectives Switch](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Switch).

**30 June–11 July 2018: PC/PS4 row ordering produces an acorn and “41”.** The 32 full-length PC rows can be reordered into a 32×32 figure, guided by slash marks at their left/right edges and interlacing/boundary continuity. A **9 July** “acorn order version” attachment and a **11 July** historical summary both demonstrate the **acorn silhouette + 41**. This is the first payoff of the original 2016 printer stream. The community does *not yet* possess a demonstrated derivation from that picture to the password. [Original operation reconstruction](historical-ordering-acorn-lineage.md), [synchronized historical printer corpora](../data/printer-reference/README.md).

**2018–2019: accepted printer passwords produce three different printed artifacts.** After the Xbox/iOS words and, later, the 2019 PC password are entered, the Playdead site generates three full-resolution artworks. The first two contain colour-coded binary: **green=1, blue=0, red=separator**, producing warnings that **factor 1** and **factor 2** security has passed. The final image embeds spectrogram/audio information. Converting the image region into sound gives spoken system narration ending with a request to access **`repo-comms-gate-98-connect`**. **The receiver, binary and image→sound transform work**; do not place the completed three-password sequence before April 2019. [Game Detectives printer/button](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Printer_Button).

**2018–2019: gate 98 turns sound into image layers and a new destination.** `/comms/gate/98/connect.html` points to a large **2048×4096** image. Selecting pixels on a regular 16-pixel lattice and isolating near-black RGB values yields colour-layer fragments; registration reveals a planet-like scan and the route **`REPO/SYS/ACTIVATE_SHUTDOWN_PROTOCOL/PROT6y723g90ty9r80234`**. This pays off in a real Terminal41 [shutdown-protocol page](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/sys/activate_shutdown_protocol/PROT6y723g90ty9r80234.html), with [confirmation](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/sys/activate_shutdown_protocol/opts/confirm_shutdown_routine.html) and commit/redirect chains. An exact source-authenticated **six-colour 2,577/2,577-pixel replay** was performed in **Oct 2026**; it is **retrospective validation**, not a new contemporary 2018 find and not an answer to the CE foreground. [GRA gate-98 audit](conjecture-lab/2026-10-08-gate98-complete-six-layer-reconstruction.md).

**2018–2020: four breached Terminal41 “schemes,” hibernation and unresolved connections.** The final status snapshot has **PLANET**, **LIFE**, **PROBE** and **CONDISCON**; the original `activate_shutdown_protocol` path gives apparent “ALL SYSTEMS SHUTDOWN / HYPERNATE” states. It is tempting to read those as four completely solved modern theories or one verified global ARG victory. Only the **presence of those labels and saved terminal state pages** is independently documented. The server-side relation between each response and each visitor's actions was not logged to the modern archive. Separate `breach_contribution_reg.html` contains long SHA-256-style hexadecimal tags and incomplete-data notices, and `saf_dat_col.html` contains a damaged bulk data source: both remain independent research targets. [printreqstatus_SD](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/sys/printreqstatus_SD.html), [breach contribution](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/dat/breach_contribution_reg.html).

**2018: YouTube stream / Anthony T. Setrinamairé.** A sometimes-sent Playdead printer email points to a named account, from which solvers locate an offline YouTube livestream. A claim that twenty concurrent viewers would trigger it is **disconfirmed by an actual 20+ observer test**. No documented payoff follows. [Game Detectives stream](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#Youtube_Stream).

## 2019: an accepted solution before its decoder, and a new physical puzzle

**3 January 2019: public state-of-play.** Microsoft's [“The As-Yet Unsolved Secret in Inside”](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2019/01/03/unsolved-secret-in-inside/) describes the collective investigation. It is a period source showing the larger endgame was still publicly open even after many printer and Terminal41 steps had progressed.

**13 April 2019: `LIFEDETECTED` is accepted, as a guess.** Someone submits this to the Playdead printer button and receives the expected password acceptance/third artifact. Crucially, the exact *mechanism* extracting it from acorn+41 is not yet known. The older Game Detectives text still says its derivation “has yet to be deciphered,” but dated 2021 Discord testimony and an authenticated 2026 video replay **supersede that specific statement**. [Game Detectives PC solution](https://wiki.gamedetectives.net/w/Inside_ARG#PC/PS4), [2021 correction](conjecture-lab/2026-10-09-cl13-2021-acorn-overlay-provenance.md).

**6–15 December 2019: mystery deliberately advertised in PS4 reversible cover.** iam8bit hints that something is hidden in the reversible PS4 art. Solvers identify one screen's **acorn** (**15 Dec**), a **planet**, graph-like drawing, other monitors and a **12:12** clock. No special in-game occurrence was found merely by visiting the relevant place at 12:12. These may be retrospective references to earlier solved mechanisms, but a one-to-one interpretation of all cover details is **unproved**. Original full-size cover scans: [inside](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/images/CE_REVERSIBLE-COVER_INSIDE_A1.JPG), [outside](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/images/CE_REVERSIBLE-COVER_INSIDE_B1.JPG); [twinysam source chronology](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#ps4-cover-hidden-mystery); [conditional four-scheme reading](conjecture-lab/2026-10-08-cover-four-schemes.md).

**12 December 2019 onward: the Collector's Edition stickers become available.** CE deliveries and making-of video reveal a **uniquely numbered seal** with two potentially useful information channels: the foreground **dot (`•`), dash (`-`), slash (`/`)** and one of **nine faint background textures** later labeled A–I. The printed number may be a serial or chain counter, but production-run-size claims are not independently established. Two distinct puzzles now share the same object. The later `/597` number is not a license to assume there are 597 total CE copies, a 648 endpoint or six exact cycles. [Original physical description](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#collectors-edition-stickers).

**16 December 2019: termination directory indexes already exist.** A saved `/sys/terminate_terminal/` directory listing includes endpoints `11`, `23`, `41`, `42` and `99` with apparent December 2019 last-modified dates. It matters to later April 2020 site archaeology, not as evidence players found or activated them at Christmas. [Preserved index](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/sys/terminate_terminal/index.html).

## 2020: the CE background is solved; a new damaged page is reached; macOS is decoded

**2 January–February 2020: sticker acquisition and A–I registration.** Early photo confirmations include **`-132` (2 Jan)**, **`•095` (5 Jan)**, **`•097` (6 Jan)** and multiple separately numbered seals from a five-item seller (**9 Jan**). On **1 Feb**, people are physically assembling image classes and explicitly use the 3×3 background order:

```text
I A B
C D E
F G H
```

With the community's convention **`A = 001`, `I = 000`**, that image class repeats every **nine serials**. The same layout is recorded **23 Feb**. On **21 Feb**, a proposed mosaic is recognized as a representation of the game's **printer**. *These are 2020 community discoveries, not projections from 2026 theory.* [twinysam chronology](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#9-piece-puzzle); [exact 2020 Discord recovery](experiment-302-historical-3x3-provenance.md).

**19 March 2020: first complete nine-tile mosaic.** Discovery of a **C-class** sticker completes the nine background classes; the resulting printer/URL image is posted. This is the **background-image assembly breakthrough**. The slash/dash/dot **foreground remains unread**. Historic Discord assembly ID: [690395145245425724](https://discord.com/channels/460626942190813184/461275582970462209/690395145245425724), [archived source reconstruction](experiment-302-historical-3x3-provenance.md).

**20–21 March 2020: hypothesis of a Terminal41 `/dat` URL.** Seeing letters beneath the sticker symbols, solvers suggest path-like values including `uat/5345rn9653f9i8nmd`. A `/dat/` directory discovery is announced on the 21st, even though the old `/dat/breach_contribution_reg` URL had been known previously. The attempted `dat/534brn9653f9i8rnd` and `...i8nmd` paths fail. This is a **partial visual reading** rather than an accepted solution.

**6 April 2020: foreground occlusion matters.** A **dash-bearing C tile** reveals characters obscured by **slash-bearing C examples**, strengthening a candidate digit `6`. This is an excellent piece of directly observable multi-layer evidence: photographs bearing the same A–I background need not have equally visible text because their independent foreground mark covers different pixels. [Dated primary record](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#9-piece-puzzle).

**20–21 April 2020: video attention, index enumeration, and the nine-tile payoff.** [oddheader's 20 Apr video](https://youtu.be/ec_8HLCboDI) attracts a newcomer who inspects a Terminal41 directory index on **21 Apr**. Among newly indexed routes are **`/sys/terminate_terminal/41/terminate.html`** and **`/dat/534brn9653f9j8mmd/`**. The latter matches the independently assembled image's URL-shaped text, fixing ambiguous characters including **`j`** and **`mmd`**. The published correct answer is **`dat/534brn9653f9j8mmd`**. This is **definitely a solved, real path and an immediate next-page payoff**; the exact byte spelling was **cross-confirmed by directory listing**, so do not claim the original blurred image alone unambiguously decoded all characters. The archived Discord TLDR posts a labeled finished “Sticker Puzzle Solution” on **28 Apr 2020**. [Primary first-person chronology](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#9-piece-puzzle); [historical source asset proof](experiment-302-historical-3x3-provenance.md).

**21 April 2020: `/sys/terminate_terminal/41/y/` and site inaccessibility.** The saved HTML asks whether to terminate terminal 41, presenting `/y/` and `/n/`. A user visits the affirmative route, and the site is thereafter apparently unavailable. The original account says the action is **believed** to have terminated the site, and a separate saved `sys/terminateall.html` exists. There is no preserved authoritative server event log establishing that one user visit actually performed a site-wide kill switch, so describe **temporal association** rather than proved causation. This episode is **distinct from** the earlier `activate_shutdown_protocol` chain. [Saved termination prompt](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/sys/terminate_terminal/41/terminate.html), [readme's explicit uncertainty](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#534brn9653f9j8mmd-puzzle).

**21 April onward: the `534brn` page adds a corrupted JPEG and `pe^!02un`.** Under the now-verified route lies HTML containing damaged **JFIF/Exif/JPEG-like bytes** that cannot be turned into an authenticated complete source image as-is. The footer includes **`pe^!02un ........ .. .`** and a reference to the terminal-41 confirmation path. Both the exact content meaning and a coherent follow-on action remain **open**; arbitrary repair of damaged image data is not a solved source decoder. [Archived page](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/terminal41.link/dat/534brn9653f9j8mmd/index.html); [source-native archaeology](conjecture-lab/2026-10-08-terminal41-unarchived-route-recovery.md).

**23–25 June 2020: macOS printer solved with an in-world poem.** Mac INSIDE appears **23 June**. **24 June** players post new printer footage; **25 June** Shikshake, Raezores and Aperson1 transcribe **16 unique** symbol lines. EitherOr proposes **overlaying a known text** on the marks and selecting letters at marked positions. Mykonos recognizes the E. E. Cummings poem already referenced in INSIDE; an exact alignment yields **`HIBERNATION IN PROGRESS REBOOT PENDING`**. This is a **successful, source-cued text overlay**, not a reason to treat any arbitrary book or poem as a plausible CE key. It thematically joins the “hibernation” terminal pages, but a contemporary new accepted server response is not documented. [Primary Mac event list and all 16 strings](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/readme.md#macos-printer-codes).

**2020–2025: sticker-owner documentation expands.** Raezores and collaborators search Reddit, Steam, Twitter, YouTube, private collecting communities, eBay, marketplaces, social-media comments and follow ownership transfers. The **recorded date** generally means *photo acquired* or *owner replied*, not sticker manufactured. Many boxes were opened with the wrapper discarded, so “lost”, “unknown” and “reported symbol without photo” have different evidentiary weight. The full per-serial acquisition chronology remains in [stickers.md](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/stickers.md), rather than being duplicated as an enormous individual-contact ledger here.

## 2021: the 2018 acorn finally pays off; the CE foreground period emerges

**21 March 2021, with an earlier 23 April 2020 precedent: sticker marks proposed as lever actions.** Archived discussion notes cover stickers/dots corresponding to bunker lever directions; the explicitly stated directional mapping is **`• = left`, `- = right`, `/ = up`**. Sorting by sticker number is proposed as an input order. This is a **well-sourced historical proposed consumer** that predates the modern H108 machine, but no authenticated replay has unlocked a new alternate version using the stickers. [Discord historical consumer audit](discord-historical-technique-inventory.md#sticker-specific-external-consumer-candidate-sleeve-geometry---lever-commands).

**25 March 2021: earliest located explicit minimum **108** sticker period.** The original community says 108 is the smallest period compatible with the known foreground marks. A modern backtest finds 2020-era observations were *already mathematically sufficient* to detect it, but that is not a discovery date: the contemporary attestation begins in March **2021**. [GRA Experiment 299](experiment-299-discord-chronology-reaudit.md).

**10 June 2021 (reported), 25 June (documented): the acorn/41 solution is actually explained.** Solver `4987372` recognizes the canonical Conway **Game of Life Acorn seed**, evolves it **41 generations**, and overlays the **102 live cells** on the **original 32×32 PC/PS4 art** at **(1,16)**. Keep original **dot** pixels *outside* the mask, and original **dash** pixels *inside* it, recoloured orange; slash pixels do not contribute. The result is exactly **90 orange cells spelling `LIFE DETECTED`**. Thus an April 2019 **guessed accepted password** received its intended causal derivation more than two years later. The 25 June community TLDR and Steam posts attest the finding. In **October 2026**, GRA recovered the actual archival 2021 demo video and independently replayed **102/102 mask sites** and **90/90 final orange sites**, zero mismatches. The source object is the **PC printer's own art**; calling the PS4 cover's tiny runner a proved extra receiver would overstate the result. [CL-16 video and pixel proof](conjecture-lab/2026-10-09-cl16-full-2021-life-overlay-replay.md), [source correction](conjecture-lab/2026-10-09-cl13-2021-acorn-overlay-provenance.md).

## Open mysteries / pitfalls requiring separate handling

1. **CE numbered foreground:** Which operation turns serial-registered `/ - •` marks (and perhaps A–I texture classes) into something a real Playdead artifact reads? The empirical H108 repetition alone is not a decrypt.
2. **`534brn9653f9j8mmd`:** Does its damaged JFIF stream have a recoverable original image? How should `pe^!02un` and the terminal-confirmation footer be parsed? Which original bytes are recoverable rather than guessed?
3. **PS4 reversible cover:** Are the four prominent monitor displays and `12:12` a self-contained decoder, a historical recap, or a cue to a different consumer? Acorn/planet recognition is only a partial interpretation.
4. **Terminal41 form/gateway:** What **accepted** response, if any, was obtained for the blank `comms_main_viewgate` input? What caused the transition from failed to successful viewgate? Are the apparent 22 characters evidence of an intended exact-length input?
5. **Terminal41 breach status, registry and safety-data collection:** Distinguish four tagged schemes from independently accepted outputs. The SHA-style `breach_contribution_reg` list, the corrupted `saf_dat_col` archive, and original missing `saf_dat_col_BACKUP` have not been exhausted as proven complete decoders.
6. **Termination causality:** The `/sys/terminate_terminal/41/y/` action precedes loss of site access in April 2020; no server-side audit proves that action *caused* the global loss or which state was intended.
7. **Other in-world inscriptions and unused cross-platform printed residues:** `A10N7`, `L08`, `B02`, `PROBE` / `CONDISCON` and other scene details can serve as independent leads without being promoted to messages by resemblance alone.
8. **Quantity and parity:** Early “no more than 500” language, a last-seen `597`, and guessed endpoints `600/621/648` are not interchangeable production facts. A parity/ECC theory needs specified encoding/check positions, not only a plausible redundancy budget.
9. **Modern candidate receivers:** Website printer POST/HTML, dormant Terminal41 pages, original game bunker lever, Steam beta password, poster/physical special-edition artwork are separate hypotheses with separate evidence requirements. No source has yet established the CE foreground goes to one of them.
