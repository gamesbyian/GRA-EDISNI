## Question

Does the fourth progress/battery-like display on the reversible Collector's Edition cover correspond to a specific pre-December-2019 INSIDE ARG artifact, as the acorn, planet, and diagnostic graph do?

This is the bounded residual question left by the post-359 cover reconstruction. It is intentionally independent of the sticker foreground machine: no POS3, selector, recursion, route, terminal, or model-filled sticker output is used to identify the display.

## Sources checked

The audit compared the repository's recovered cover history against the upstream `twinysam/INSIDE-ARG` chronology and the Game Detectives INSIDE ARG chronology, including the PC/PS4 acorn, Xbox/planet, Terminal41 diagnostic/status material, and shutdown sequence. Searches also combined INSIDE / Terminal41 with battery, progress, progress bar, and the diagnostic label.

## Result

**No independent pre-December-2019 artifact was identified that can presently be matched to the fourth progress/battery-like cover display.**

The public histories strongly recover the other three screen references:

1. PC/PS4 acorn;
2. Xbox / Terminal41 planet;
3. Terminal41 diagnostic graph.

They also preserve Terminal41 status/progress language, including system checks and a reported `98%` status line, but current evidence does not establish that any such status display is the same visual object as the fourth cover monitor. Textual thematic similarity is insufficient for identification.

The upstream cover chronology records closeups of the acorn, planet, graph, and another screen, but does not document a solved identity for that fourth screen.

## Interpretation

This is a **bounded negative**, not evidence that the fourth display is novel.

At the current evidence boundary, three of four displays are independently tied to the pre-existing ARG, while the fourth remains unresolved. No externally forced transform, selector, index, or registration rule has emerged from it. The cover therefore still does not reopen terminal-`100`, G6, G7, or another foreground-machine semantic lane.

The cheapest live explanation remains that the cover is an ARG bridge/recap with one screen whose exact historical referent has not been recovered. A stronger claim requires either the original source asset for the fourth display, a contemporaneous comparison identifying it, or a pre-December-2019 artifact with a distinctive matching visual form.

## Reopening rule

Do not broaden this into visual fishing. Reopen only if one of these appears:

1. a higher-resolution or original cover source that exposes distinctive structure;
2. a contemporaneous Discord/forum post explicitly identifying the fourth display;
3. a preserved pre-December-2019 ARG image with a visually specific match;
4. creator/iam8bit material describing the four-screen clue.

Otherwise treat the cover-specific semantic lane as exhausted at current evidence.
