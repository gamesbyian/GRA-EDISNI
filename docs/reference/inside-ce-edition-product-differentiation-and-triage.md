## First-party evidence: two products, reused marketing context

There is a material difference between **the standalone INSIDE PS4 physical game** and **the INSIDE Collector's Edition**, even though both are limited editions, sold by iam8bit and associated with Playdead:

| Attribute | Standalone PS4 physical game | Full Collector's Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Official listing | [INSIDE PS4 Physical Game](https://www.iam8bit.com/products/inside-ps4-physical-game) | [INSIDE Collector's Edition](https://www.iam8bit.com/products/inside-collector-s-edition) |
| Price shown on first-party listing | **USD 29.99** | **USD 375.00** |
| Production quantity explicitly stated on current first-party listing | **Limited edition of 2,000** | **Limited edition**, no numerical production total on this page |
| Listed contents | Physical PS4 game + foldout art poster | PS4 game; silicone RealDoll-assisted Huddle with stand; miniature Boy; art cards; foldout poster; premium presentation box |
| Relevance to numbered ARG stickers | **No first-party promise of numbered CE sticker** | Physical edition under study; verify any individual serial with original numbered wrapper/photo |

**Crucial distinction:** the **2,000 figure belongs to the standalone PS4 game listing**. It is not evidence of the full CE's run size, nor a refutation or proof of a ~600-piece CE serial range. Keep the product populations separate in production-count and parity modeling.

The standalone product description explicitly mentions its connection to the Collector's Edition and calls itself “perhaps, more than it seems.” This is official marketing copy, not proof that the standalone copies hide CE stickers or a second code. Consider it an optional product/ARG search premise, **not** a constraint or a solved clue.

The standalone store listing's imagery also includes a lifestyle photograph with an \`InsideCE_Lifestyle_...\` asset-name lineage (for example \`InsideCE_Lifestyle_00013.jpg\`). A generic image search, image filename, poster, or photo of a PS4 case can therefore lead to **the wrong product class** even when the imagery originates in official iam8bit promotional material. A generic huddle sculpture photo is stronger CE context but still might be a reused marketing image rather than proof a social-media author purchased a copy.

An independent [Cheap Ass Gamer discussion from April 22, 2020](https://www.cheapassgamer.com/threads/indie-limited-obscure-physical-release-deals-and-discussion-thread.357745/page-133) explicitly identifies iam8bit selling the **standalone** PS4 version for $29.99 alongside the much costlier CE. Date and source make this a historical **negative control** for misread receipt claims.

### Recommendations for search and image provenance

1. Search for native-language names and descriptions of the **full package** (Huddle, silicone/RealDoll sculpture, presentation box, art-card fan, sealed outer shipping wrapper, CE-specific sticker), not “limited PS4 physical edition” alone.
2. Never infer the full CE or sticker ownership from a $29.99 listing, a standard PS4 case image, a collector label generated by a price-tracking website, or the **2,000-copy** standalone limit.
3. Distinguish **publisher promotion**, **unboxing of a physically possessed CE**, **later reseller photo**, **known physical sticker photo**, and **AI/search thumbnail**. A YouTube reviewer may use publisher footage, and a public seller may show multiple photographs of *one* transferred CE.
4. Read text *on the actual wrapper* and its source chronology before creating new sticker observations. Filename, source alt text, search title, and apparent digits in blurry thumbnails do not establish serial, polarity or background.
5. Treat simultaneous multiple full CEs as possible but exceptional: the canonical [sticker ledger](https://github.com/twinysam/INSIDE-ARG/blob/master/stickers.md) already contains explicitly documented two-copy cases. Prefer individual sticker/wrapper evidence over the number of accounts or listing posts.

## Newly implemented: index-result triage without evidence inflation

The [offline script](../../scripts/triage_inside_ce_index_results.py) reads one or more **successful licensed web-index result files**, consolidates exact URL variants, preserves Reddit *comment* identities distinct from their shared *thread* context, maps YouTube video/comment links and eBay item IDs, cross-references URLs with the [existing 23-row historical CE leads ledger](../../data/reference/inside-ce-owner-search-ledger-2026-10-12.tsv), and flags possible standalone false positives.

It emits a **review queue** with distinct status fields:

- \`exact_ledger_url\`: normalized original URL is already listed in the research ledger. Not a new find.
- \`same_thread_only_no_independent_owner_proof\`: thread shares a topic/ID with a ledger record, but another comment may describe a *different person or copy*. Must inspect exact comment.
- \`not_in_local_ledger_unreconciled\`: new URL relative to this **local** ledger. It may still be in twinysam's sticker list, a prior research note, an archive mirror, or another alias.
- \`possible_standalone_ps4_false_positive\`: snippet has a strongly misleading product-class phrase, not a determination that its seller *did not* own a CE.
- \`index_candidate_url_only\` and \`unreviewed_source_content\`: index results are not independent ownership testimony.

**Always \`new_confirmed_stickers=0\`.** No keyword or numerical pattern can upgrade a hit to a physical-sticker finding. The existing physical sticker ledger and experimental model remain untouched.

Search engine snippets and titles are potentially hostile or inaccurate content. The script truncates snippets, rejects invalid URL schemes and malformed provider result shapes, and exports formula-safe TSV. It preserves original result links for source checking but does not fetch, crawl, message or download any author data.

Run offline after a bounded index campaign:

\`\`\`bash
python scripts/triage_inside_ce_index_results.py \
  --input search-output/inside-ce-index-results.jsonl \
  --ledger data/reference/inside-ce-owner-search-ledger-2026-10-12.tsv \
  --canonical-stickers /research/twinysam-INSIDE-ARG/stickers.md \
  --output search-output/inside-ce-index-candidates.tsv \
  --summary search-output/inside-ce-index-triage-summary.json

python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_inside_ce_index_triage.py -v
\`\`\`

The [GitHub Actions owner campaign](../../.github/workflows/inside-ce-native-search-campaign.yml) now runs this **offline triage after an explicitly authorized API batch**, publishing a candidate TSV and a summary alongside the original returned URLs and search manifest metadata.

### Community-canonical link cross-check

As an optional second source of *already documented URLs*, the offline triager accepts `--canonical-stickers /path/to/twinysam-INSIDE-ARG/stickers.md`. It extracts public `http(s)` links from the existing community chronology, normalizes source and thread IDs with the same method used for fresh hits, and stores the canonical file's **SHA-256** in the output summary. The GHA owner-search workflow obtains this public file through a separate read-only checkout when index searches are explicitly enabled. No API call or owner outreach is needed for URL comparison.

`canonical_exact_source_url` means a result links the same normalized source URL already present in the community's sticker ledger. `canonical_thread_context_only` means a different result links the same Reddit thread or YouTube video, possibly a **different person or physical CE**. `not_in_canonical_link_list_or_not_provided` is only an absence from extracted **links**, not proof the CE is unknown to the community; accounts may appear as text, seller names or private-contact notes. The triager never tries to infer identity from aliases or automatically assign a serial.

**Known limitations:** canonical ledger normalization recognizes common Reddit permalinks, YouTube watch/comment identifiers, item-number eBay URLs and query-tracking parameters. It does not prove usernames are the same person, that two eBay item IDs represent two physical CEs, that an old Reddit shortlink resolves to a specific comment, or that a missed result does not exist. Social-site live/API permission and rights remain separate. No full-text archive acquisition or native-language 23,499-query campaign is claimed here.
