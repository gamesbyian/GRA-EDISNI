# Old LIMBO web-site provenance and alternative media mirrors

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Mode:** DISCOVER; source-forensics and recovery, not a sticker mechanism. Extends [the historical domain inventory](playdead-historical-web-provenance-2026-10-16.md) without treating independently hosted websites as Playdead-owned.

## Highest-confidence new finding: the original LIMBO site implementers

[Twentyfour's direct LIMBO project case study](https://twentyfour.dk/en/cases/limbo/) (with independent [Danish version](https://twentyfour.dk/cases/limbo/)) says design agency **Plastiik** approached **Twentyfour, formerly HTML24**, to collaborate on the LIMBO website. Plastiik and Playdead designed it; Twentyfour implemented it. Twentyfour explicitly records WordPress, Flash, and PayPal and enumerates screenshot gallery, news, press handling, payments and retailer integrations. This independently corroborates the existence of the WordPress infrastructure inferred from an [August 10, 2011 contemporary image embed](https://unknowns.de/forum/thread/3853-aktuell-gespielte-videospiele/?pageNo=2): `http://limbogame.org/wordpress2/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/screenshot05-800x450.jpg`.

**Ownership:** `twentyfour.dk` and `plastiik.com` are externally operated vendor websites; their relationship to a commissioned web project does **not** make them Playdead-owned domains. [Plastiik's contemporary corporate site](https://plastiik.com/) indicates continued presence, but this investigation did not find a first-party LIMBO case there. Do not infer its latest service offerings existed when the site was built. The '2342 days / 9653 lines' numbers on the modern case page have no independently verified accounting meaning and are not game-code clues.

**Follow-ups (no outreach):** locate historical `html24.dk` and `plastiik.com` portfolio screenshots/links around 2010–2013, any public source/site screenshots, and original site's HTML/WordPress media URL list. Do not scan vendor infrastructure beyond public indexed pages. Do not confuse the unrelated design studio [limbo.works](https://limbo.works/) with Playdead.

## A new early corporate email-name source (historical association only)

The [World Summit Award Cairo 2013 catalogue](https://www.fantrust.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/WSA_cairo_final_catalogue-2013.pdf) lists Playdead with `fan@playdeadgames.com` and `www.limbogame.org`. Separately, [Playdead's June 2013 Vita press release](https://pressreleases.triplepointpr.com/2013/06/04/playdead-publishes-limbo-on-playstation-vita/) names `press@playdeadgames.com`, and a [contemporary October 2013 PlayStation support quotation](https://www.pocketgamer.com/limbo/playdead-confirms-that-limbo-is-no-longer-a-ps3-vita-cross-buy-game/) names `support@playdeadgames.com`. These show domain use beyond an obsolete marketing URL but **do not establish any mailbox remains operational**, nor that its users were ARG participants. Do not contact these addresses.
