# Playdead historical web footprint: first-party links, external hosts and archival recovery

_16 October 2026 | DISCOVER / source-first archival investigation, **not** a new sticker-cipher hypothesis._

## Executive result

Playdead's first-party historical presence is not limited to `playdead.com`. Contemporaneous Danish industry and official press sources establish earlier corporate websites and branding, and the [official Playdead GitHub publications README](https://github.com/playdeadgames/publications/blob/master/INSIDE/readme.md) **directly links to independently hosted developer sites**, alongside its own legacy INSIDE URL. A 2011 historical forum post additionally preserves a direct path into LIMBO's earlier WordPress installation.

No new Playdead-authored ARG endpoint, decoded sticker payload or undisclosed owned domain has been confirmed. Distinguish **domain ownership**, **first-party publication**, **developer-authored outside hosting**, **contemporaneous third-party testimony**, and **independent ARG/community mirrors**. Corporate association is not a WHOIS claim.

## Independent 2018 metadata cross-reference: `printer.jpg`

A [June 4, 2018 Steam conversation](https://steamcommunity.com/app/304430/discussions/0/359543951720753445?ctp=53&l=latam) directly reports that mysterious binary rejection emails sometimes attached `printer.jpg`, at different small sizes (1.1 KB versus 7.3 KB). Participants say the image contained metadata naming **Andreas Normand Grøntved** and an image-software marker; one participant explicitly says “nvm it's just the exif data” and supplies [their screenshot](https://i.imgur.com/mjttLLJ.png).

Independently, Grøntved is the named author of [The Starfish Rig](https://blog.playdead.com/articles/StarfishRig/StarfishRig.html) on Playdead's own site, with technical material hosted under `sota.dk`. Thus the **name-to-studio relationship is externally established** and the metadata claim is genuinely relevant to Playdead provenance. But the actual native `printer.jpg` bytes, dual-size comparison, original metadata extraction and image-source lineage have **not** been reverified in this pass. The source was posted in 2018, so do not label it an unknown community discovery. Avoid attributing design of the broader ARG to the named contributor solely from EXIF.

**High-leverage next experiment:** seek unchanged original 1.1 KB and 7.3 KB attachments in historical public archives, collect hashes and full metadata and verify whether the size difference is recompression versus changed pixels. Do not ask private owners for their emails; any recovery must come from public voluntary archives. If originals cannot be recovered, close as unverified eyewitness metadata.
