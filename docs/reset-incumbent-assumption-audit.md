## The main epistemic bottleneck

The current machine is impressively constrained **after** G1, G3, G5, G6 and G7 are admitted. The reset therefore should spend very little effort proving more consequences inside that family until those five moves have been independently motivated or beaten by rivals.

This matters because several strong-looking facts are conditional:

- "25 of 27 primary trits are forced" means forced **under POS3**.
- "Q4 reconstructs to a selector scaffold" means reconstructed **inside the exceptional-depth ternary family plus recursive closure**.
- "route shell 120/012/102 is unique" means unique **under the three-distinct-permutation criterion**.
- "terminal 100 emerges" means emerges **inside the selected recursive operation families**.

Those remain real results. They do not erase the cost of entering the family.

## Historical support actually available

### For the 108 / 12x3x3 organization

Support is good.

Community history independently reached:

- the 108 foreground repeat;
- 12x9 renderings;
- twelve 3x3 squares;
- the 81+27 split;
- nine slash/dash blocks plus three slash/dot blocks.

This substantially lowers the hindsight cost of the frame organization.

### For POS3 itself

Support is weaker.

The human-solve reconstruction identifies this as the weakest recognition step. Later experiments show physical columns are uniquely successful among natural straight-rail partitions and generalize on holdout observations, which is useful evidence **within local positional-code families**.

Missing piece: a historical or external Playdead cue saying the exceptional position of a three-cell rail is itself the intended symbol value.

### For Q4 as selector

There is a plausible physical story once exceptional-depth coding is accepted: the final 27 cells naturally form three layers over nine A-I positions.

Missing piece: an independent cue that the tail should *address* the body rather than, for example, order, mask, validate, or overlay it.

### For recursive reuse

Playdead precedent supports reuse of earlier outputs as selectors or keys. The Terminal 41 RGB work is an example.

Missing piece: a cue for **this exact self-application**. "Reuse exists elsewhere" is weaker than "reuse this selector twice here."

### For route semantics / G7

This is the most conceptually expensive downstream filter.

The three-distinct-reversible-map criterion is compact and productive, but current historical evidence does not yet show that the sticker foreground was presented as a routing/permutation problem.

Until a route cue is found, G7 should be treated as an elegant model selector, not as author-intent evidence.

## Reset attack order

### A. Attack G1 before more downstream uniqueness work

Reproduce all historically attested frame/row/column organizations on observation-only data.

Compare POS3 against:

- boundary-constrained spatial assembly;
- repeated-frame/motif continuation;
- binary or ternary occupancy without positional value;
- row-position rather than column-position coding;
- ordering supplied by A-I/background geometry.

Use held-out observations whenever possible.

### B. Attack G3 independently of G1

Treat the 27 slash/dot tail as an object in its own right.

Enumerate low-description roles that fit solved-ARG precedent:

- nine 3-way values;
- three 9-cell masks;
- ordering metadata;
- checksum/validation layer;
- overlay/select layer;
- pointer into A-I background image;
- operation cue.

Do not use terminal `100` or route survival as the first filter.

### C. Audit G5/G6 against historical language

Search archived sticker discussions for verbs and analogies that imply:

- choose/select;
- depth/layer;
- address/index;
- overlay;
- use bottom on top;
- repeat/apply again;
- same layout/order.

A pre-machine community statement of the operation would radically change its prior.

### D. Demand a route cue before privileging G7

Search cross-puzzle and sticker-adjacent artifacts for a native reason to care about:

- permutations;
- three routes;
- invertibility;
- one route per state/class;
- `120/012/102`-like mappings.

Absent such a cue, compare G7-selected models to equally compact non-route criteria.

## Cheap falsifiers for the incumbent

The incumbent should be considered materially strengthened by any of:

1. a historical pre-model sticker message proposing exceptional-position ternary reading;
2. an external ARG artifact independently specifying selection by A-I depth;
3. a held-out sticker/background observation predicted uniquely by POS3/selector recursion and missed by simpler rivals;
4. a solved neighboring puzzle using the same "three-position exceptional member = value" encoding;
5. a route/permutation cue tied directly to the sticker/background artifact.

It should lose relative confidence if:

1. a simpler historically motivated operation predicts the same held-out cells;
2. the 81/27 split yields an independently recognizable object without POS3;
3. a boundary/order reconstruction explains why the A-I layer and foreground coexist;
4. G7 is the only thing preventing many equally compact alternatives.

## Working conclusion

The reset does **not** currently invalidate the machine. It changes where the burden of proof sits.

The machine's downstream mathematics is mature. The research frontier is now upstream: why a human should choose G1, G3, G5, G6 and especially G7 from the physical/historical puzzle, before knowing what those choices eventually produce.
