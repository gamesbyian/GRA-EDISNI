## Six rules for using this portfolio

1. Treat physical serials, symbol observations, the period-9 picture carrier, known 108 repeat and 81/27 vocabulary separately from derived Q4 grammar, state 100 and guessed image.
2. Distinguish the nine background CLASSES from nine occurrences WITHIN ONE class: Experiment 319 shows that transferring their coordinates needs a separate authorial map.
3. For every bitmap hypothesis, use the directly observed original artwork pixels, track support masks, freeze shifts before scoring and quantify best-of-many orientations.
4. For any source-assisted receiving target, recover the artifact and input grammar before using it to select a sticker completion. Do not retrofit 534brn or Terminal41 merely because nine numbers/slots look right.
5. Use same observed-residue masks (66 of 108 known in most recent audited corpus) and physical holdouts; completion-model fantasies are demonstrations only.
6. Document at least one matched null, a baseline, and an explicit failure gate. Historical attempts are inspiration, not independent confirmation.

### The crucial methodological distinction

A failed end-to-end readable output need not falsify a proposed *intermediate* selector, order, repair instruction, or partial identifier. Conversely, a mechanism that becomes interesting only after guessing its receiver has gained no independent confirmation. In the **DEVELOP** track, provisionally grant a candidate its smallest explicit unsupported premises and derive consequences without early dismissal. In the **VALIDATE** track, count only source-authored mappings, physical holdouts, predeclared rival predictions, and matched controls. Never migrate a fitted parameter silently between tracks.

For the first six serious candidates, maintain a one-page **conditional complete-solve sketch**: collection/ordering of stickers; precise symbol-to-operation mapping; source artifact/consumer; proposed human-executable steps; the actual expected output type (including partial/noisy identifiers); next in-world or web action; unsupported assumptions; minimal discriminating observation; and what makes the scenario impossible. Include the **null narrative**: the foreground may be self-contained, manufacturing metadata, or an abandoned/closed continuation, with no active modern endpoint.
