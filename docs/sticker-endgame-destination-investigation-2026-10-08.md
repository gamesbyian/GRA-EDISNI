# INSIDE ARG sticker mystery: destination-first endgame investigation

_Date: 2026-10-08. Nature: research synthesis, not a purported sticker solution._
_Branch purpose: endgame hypotheses, provenance, discriminating tests. Does not modify physical observations, completion models, or experiment ledger._

## Executive synthesis

**Leading interpretation:** the CE foreground is more likely to be an **interface with another puzzle component** (selection, key, registration, short command, or next-stage access instruction) than a freestanding narrative plaintext or literal high-resolution artwork. This is an evidence-weighted design inference, **not a decoder**. The CE background has already connected a nine-fragment physical mosaic directly to the Terminal41 address **dat/534brn9653f9j8mmd**. Xbox, iOS, Switch, PC and Terminal41 repeatedly show output-to-next-consumer architecture. A cover independently advertised as hiding a clue points back to several older ARG artifacts.

Most actionable endgame candidates:

1. a short operation/key for the damaged Terminal41 JPEG or its surviving page;
2. a registration or composition instruction connecting the CE foreground, the reversible PS4 cover and printer/planet/diagnostics artifacts;
3. a mission-state/status/shutdown/reboot access token, rather than a literal image;
4. a three-way physical input/lever sequence, **but not** the known bunker code by direct cyclic reading.

Keep a genuine null: the sticker foreground could lead only to a self-contained answer or to an unavailable/decommissioned online artifact. A thematic Project 3 reveal is a credible downstream *reward type*, not an identified foreground payload.

## Source-quality and epistemic rules

Levels here are **directly observed**, **historically documented**, **model-conditional**, **candidate inference**, and **speculative**. Community repetition is not independent corroboration. Published reconstructions of Game of Life and macOS may depend on later research; archival chronology must be distinguished from mechanism demonstrations. The Game Detectives page says PC LIFEDETECTED was *guessed*, but a June 2021 Steam post says the acorn method was subsequently recovered. The newer code-level Reddit audit contests whether the macOS poem overlay produces the claimed phrase; absence of a decoder in the game binary would not refute an intended *external* poem overlay. Treat the macOS interpretation as requiring independent reproduction, not as a verified implementation.

The existing elaborate ternary machine, POS3 grammar, G5/G6, 14 states, route patterns, and terminal 100 are **conditional model results**, not incoming evidence for the endgame. Recent unmerged PRs #140-143 contain rival-grammar and cube controls. Their results are recorded for context but are not silently promoted to main-branch truth.

## Physical sticker constraints and new destination-oriented audit

Current canonical file data/observations.csv contains **84** sticker records, **66** unique H108 residues, **54** known in residues 1..81, **12** known in 82..108, and **42** missing residues (27+15). Sixteen repeat groups generate 20 agreeing symbol pairs and zero disagreements. This independently supports H108; it does not verify how to read it.

Each of the first 81 cells currently belongs to the binary alphabet slash/dash; the last 27 to slash/dot. **Conditional on that sector split being exact**, the 108-cell *foreground-only* symbol channel has at most 108 bits of raw combinatorial information, i.e. 13.5 bytes, and the 42 still unobserved cells at most 42 independent bits, i.e. 5.25 bytes. Constraints such as one-per-column reduce possibilities further. The ceiling excludes background pixels, serial/manufacturing side-information, timing, and references to external material. It cannot exclude a tiny pictogram or an efficient seed/compressed representation. But an uncompressed monochrome 128x128 bitmap alone needs **16,384 bits**, over 151 times that foreground ceiling. An elaborate image/sound reveal consequently almost certainly involves an external artifact, index, generator, or substantial prior structure.

The nine ASCII digits in the CE background's known path are **534965398**. They use only six distinct numerals, repeat 3, 5, and 9, and omit 1, 2 and 7. Thus they are **not a simple 1..9 permutation**, although they could still be repeated indices, a route, base conversion input, or cryptic key.

The proposed cover/sleeve mapping is **dot=L, dash=R, slash=U**, based on preserved April-2020 to March-2021 Discord discussions. In the known H108 order, the first 81 would have only {U,R} commands and the last 27 only {U,L}. Exhaustively checking the 108 cyclic placements of **UURLRRRUUURLLL**, and its reverse, permits **zero** locations even if unknown marks take arbitrary values within their actual zone alphabets. This independently rules out the literal known bunker password as a contiguous sticker tape under that mapping. It does *not* rule out an externally specified selection, new sequence, or cover-as-legend rather than lever-input claim. Existing Experiment 343 independently reaches the same result.

The same information-budget script is deliberately incapable of producing a decoded phrase, image or guessed sticker: scripts/audit_endgame_channel_budget.py.

## Hypothesis portfolio

### H1. The stickers provide a key/selector for the known damaged CE-linked JPEG

**A/B: Proposition and visible destination.** Successful solving yields an image dimension, byte-mask/ordering instruction, fragment index, error-repair key or address permitting recovery/interpretation of the damaged image; the *ultimate* reveal is the content of that image or a link derived from it.

**C/E evidence and genealogy.** The sticker's solved backgrounds deliver the Terminal41 path. The page has a corrupted JPEG and footer. Experiments 413-417 recover the footer **128 UNSOLVED**, JPEG SOF dimension range 128..255, baseline sampling and scan boundaries; Experiment 423 shows all 43 geometrically possible MCU counts survive the lossy entropy constraints. Historical Terminal41 puzzles used outputs to filter image pixels and reveal routes.

**D mechanism.** One of 108 binary-ish foreground bits might indicate orientation, choose a transformation/byte interpretation or index a recovery artifact. A small sticker key cannot repair the large destroyed entropy stream by itself, unless the damage is constrained by a separate source or redundant representation.

**F weaknesses.** Neither a key-ingress interface nor a known byte-to-sticker alignment exists. 128 is a good dimension clue, **not** proof of 128x128. The JPEG loss is too extensive for literal foreground-byte recovery.

**G/H discriminators/tests.** Identify an independently present 108-, 27-, nine-, 3x3-, or serial-addressed structure in *the preserved page*, JPEG metadata, original uncorrupted asset variants or historical display. Freeze an alignment before applying model ensembles. If no native index/interface exists, downgrade this to thematic adjacency. Seek alternate archival *pre-corruption* capture; do not invent JPEG bytes.

**I appraisal.** Strongest **specific physical-to-web continuation**, moderate confidence in destination family, low confidence in a concrete repair method.

### H2. The stickers complete a cross-artifact registration/composite on the reversible cover

**A/B.** A sticker-selected layer, subset, alignment or ordering instructs how to combine the cover's acorn, planet, diagnostic and progress displays with original printer/Terminal41 material. Output might be a previously concealed shape, text, image, or path.

**C/E.** The cover is expressly advertised as clue-bearing, and source comparisons identify at least three screens as references to earlier ARG products; the acorn display is partial. This is independent of contemporary sticker-machine modelling. The Xbox and CE backgrounds both demonstrate image-first spatial assembly; the acorn puzzle demonstrates overlay and generation.

**D.** Rather than place 108 bits directly onto an arbitrary cover pixel grid, use specific monitor contents as externally fixed registration anchors, then test whether sticker class/position controls which canonical prior artifact is visible or how it is composited.

**F.** No independent sticker-to-screen correspondence or valid scale/offset has been established. Four monitors are not nine A-I slots. Existing cover mirror/blend attempts are negative.

**G/H.** Recover exact monitor crops, their source-image identity, affine registration and *differences*, blind to sticker foreground. A repeatable omitted patch or uniquely specified visual transform would be positive evidence. Otherwise no free-grid optimisation.

**I.** High-confidence **unresolved adjacent clue**, medium-confidence same-ARG bridge, low-confidence foreground consumer until registration is found.

### H3. The foreground is an authentication/status transition for the mission system

**A/B.** The output is a short authorization phrase, protocol ID, selector, state transition or sequence of submissions. Success would move Terminal41 from hold/shutdown to resumed scan, reboot, or another stage, perhaps exposing a new PDF/asset.

**C/E.** Website print requests are demonstrated input-to-reward operations; Terminal41 reports security factors, mission hold, shutdown procedures and gateway auth. Mac's claimed reboot text may fit the narrative but its exact derivation remains disputed. Official Xbox Wire in January 2019 independently documented the subscription-box input and PDF response.

**D.** CE symbols could derive a finite command, selected word, protocol token, 3-state decision sequence, or route key. The serial code might function like an authorization share distributed across owners, not message characters.

**F.** Original backend no longer reliably accessible; historical Terminal41 status logs are not necessarily caused by submitted guesses. No physical sticker-derived password has been authenticated.

**G/H.** Reconstruct exact original form/JS request grammar, historical printed state and route dependencies from archives. Verify whether a 3-way, nine-slot, 27-index, or 108-bit input grammar ever existed. An ordinary status page with no such ingress weakens the claim.

**I.** Strong **reward/interface precedent**; medium plausible destination, currently no literal testable candidate password.

### H4. The stickers encode a three-way interaction with an in-game mechanism

**A/B.** A successful decode yields lever movements or another physical/controller input sequence that unlocks an Easter egg, alternative scene, sound, or simply confirms the ARG solution.

**C/E.** Historical Discord independently related sleeve symbol geometry to the bunker lever: dot left, dash right, slash up. Switch printed code triggered controller attach/detach operation. The secret-ending lever is a genuine three-choice puzzle.

**D.** Foreground supplies the command alphabet, while another artifact supplies start, end, subset, order or grouping. Could be interpreted as a visual legend for an entirely different three-state operation.

**F.** The **existing** bunker password cannot appear contiguously as described above; no new untested input point is established. Treat dormant code signals, cut content and alternate combinations as leads, not proof of accessible content.

**G/H.** Search retail code for genuine consumers beyond the known SecretJoystick validator, inspect any alternative password tables and test only a source-specified sequence. Compare the archived cover orientation with the symbol legend. Never brute-force arbitrary lever strings as confirmation.

**I.** Strong provenance for symbol-to-operation *semantics*, low-to-medium for a new in-game endpoint.

### H5. The foreground is an index/book cipher across nine classes and existing material

**A/B.** Output is a collection of selected characters, pixels, frames, file offsets, paragraphs, tones or timestamps from another known document/asset. Result might be a short password or second-stage route.

**C/E.** Nine A-I backgrounds and H108 repetition are physically supported. The solved background yields nine digits; historical 2025 notes proposed using them to order/index sticker sections. iOS hour ordering, macOS proposed poem cutout, and Terminal41 exact-colour filtering supply the general prior for metadata indexing.

**D.** Treat class as one axis, serial cycle/depth as another and symbols as choose/skip/index information. Preserve 9x12 and 12x9 as *representations*, not authorially proved read directions. Require target material to supply record geometry and an explicit index convention.

**F.** The address digits cannot be a nine-way permutation unaided. No independent target of the right length/registration has been identified. Repeated scanning of old plaintext for near-matches risks selection bias.

**G/H.** Inventory candidate target domains and exact lengths; preregister index-origin, orientation and symbol meaning. Test complete physical-compatible candidate ensemble, never only a visually appealing completion.

**I.** Good data-carrier economy and high historical plausibility, but many degrees of freedom until a real target is found.

### H6. A compact generative seed or reconstruction recipe

**A/B.** The foreground carries a small seed, boundary condition, finite-state program, generation count, compositing mask or traversal. Applying the instructions to a known image or cellular automaton yields the *real* larger image/message.

**C/E.** PC acorn+41 to Game of Life generation and overlay is a documented designer precedent; the 108-bit information upper bound makes generation/pointer methods far more plausible than literal large images.

**D.** Specific recovered words, numbers or visual features must first identify *which generator* and its parameters. The old acorn 41 could only transfer if an external cue makes that deliberate.

**F.** No such foreground-native cue has been found. A large algorithm search over reversible/compressive transforms is not evidence.

**G/H.** Audit surviving CE and Terminal41 surfaces for author-supplied operation names/parameters/iteration counts; run only those preregistered simulations against all candidate completions, with shuffle controls.

**I.** Mechanistically credible but currently speculative as the *actual* sticker reading.

### H7. The foreground gives a route to an audiovisual Project 3/planet teaser

**A/B.** A decoded command, key, URL or mask ultimately reveals a soundscape, spectrogram, transmission, planet/probe chart, short video frame, or development artwork rather than a textual answer.

**C/E.** The historical printer chain produced noisy PDF imagery, audio extracted from a spectrogram, mission/probe rhetoric and material reported as art for Playdead's next game. The ARG can change carrier types.

**D.** Small symbolic output selects or unlocks large external media. A direct 108-cell sound file or detailed bitmap is information-poor; pointing to one is realistic.

**F.** The 2018 print stage had already offered imagery and sound, so a new sticker stage need not culminate in another. Claims that release timing awaits an ARG solve are unsubstantiated community speculation.

**G/H.** Identify **unaccounted-for** referenced media assets, unused printable routes or independently recoverable hashes with a compatible input surface. If all candidate audio/images predate CE with no missing access gate, downgrade.

**I.** Moderate confidence in an audiovisual *ultimate reward class*, low confidence in precise sticker mechanism.

### H8. The Xbox planet's otherwise ignored Braille numerals form a second-layer index

**A/B.** The nine-state residual numeral alphabet supplies labels/indices allowing the later sticker A-I classes to be read, or the sticker reveals how to interpret those digits.

**C/E.** Experiments 368-369 document 67 decimal Braille-ASCII cells with **no 5**, leaving nine distinct digit symbols; the independently solved letter-bearing orientation uniquely produces that nine-value support among rectangle symmetries. The later CE uses nine physical background classes.

**D.** Exact registration/paired coordinate between Braille cell position and CE tile or fragment is required **before** a digit-to-A-I bijection. A common target could provide this.

**F.** These are Braille-ASCII glyph identities, not necessarily numbers; absence of 5 could be an occupancy artifact. No paired alignment is known. Pure nine-to-nine coincidence is cheap.

**G/H.** Inspect 2018 historical discussion for whether numeric cells were called filler/unresolved; reconstruct planet cell coordinates and any externally visible second coordinate. Do not brute-force all 9! mappings and report an attractive hit.

**I.** Intriguing precisely constrained **cross-ARG alternative**, but low support for a causal bridge.

### H9. Standalone, partly deliberately incomplete, or historically decommissioned puzzle

**A/B.** The solution might be a compact confirmation image, self-referential message, manufacturing trick or proof that the community collectively reconstructed a pattern. Its endpoint might have been retired or unavailable by the time modern solvers recovered it.

**C/E.** The background already provides one complete answer, while the foreground remains physically sparse. The online site has lost availability; even completed historical ARG stages can contain dead UI/unused strings. The puzzle might celebrate communal reconstruction rather than feed a long narrative payoff.

**D.** Serial distribution, repetition, pooled partials and a small canonical artifact could themselves constitute the intended interaction.

**F.** Explicit ARG-style symbol design and 108-cycle/sector distinction argue against pure decoration. No author statement identifies the foreground as a deliberate null or unfinishable challenge.

**G/H.** Find contemporaneous production notes, alternate sticker print sheets or identical sealed-packaging designs establishing whether the foreground was pre-generated with a specific intended readout. Any actual external consumer contradicts the standalone premise.

**I.** Required adversarial control; not currently the preferred explanation, but keeps the analysis from presupposing a grand finale.

## New tests and negatives from this pass

- **Information budget:** foreground sector-compatible upper bound 108 bits; known 66 cells, 42 free cells. A literal 128x128 one-bit bitmap requires 16,384 bits. Thus a full image/audio endpoint needs extraordinary compression, an external asset or generative procedure.
- **Address digit audit:** 534965398 contains only six distinct numerals, three repeats (3/5/9), no 1/2/7. Rules out *literal* unique-class permutation interpretation.
- **Known bunker password:** 0 of 108 possible starts under the demonstrated two-zone symbol alphabet; 0 in the reverse direction. Independent reproduction of the stronger negative recorded by Experiment 343, not a new discovery.
- **H108 provenance check:** 84 records, 66 residues, 20 agreeing physical repeat pairs; 0 disagreement. No sticker was guessed or imputed.
- **Negative results inherited, not rerun:** Experiments 345/360 fail to validate free row sorting; 403-405 close direct Morse; 408 closes the Terminal41 nine-page countdown as a nine-value consumer; 419 finds XOR-derived weights conditional on the machine itself; 423 rules out reconstructing JPEG MCU count from surviving lossy entropy; #140 punch-card transform adds no information; #142 cubes at chance; #143 enumeration is a competing-grammar, not semantic, result.
- **Source contradiction:** macOS poem decode challenged by 2026 decompilation post; current pass has not independently reproduced or refuted that poster's code analysis. Do not collapse community retelling into verified mechanism.
- No new plaintext, URL, image, or game secret was established. No lost physical symbols were filled.

## Recommended discriminating evidence, in descending information gain

1. **Exact cover screen-to-source diff**, performed from archived high-res scans without consulting sticker completions; does it independently define an alignment, missing piece or operation? This tests H2 and can inform H1/H5/H6.
2. **CE Terminal41 damaged page input/metadata audit:** inventory *all* known preserved source variants and image/header/footer; verify whether a sticker-sized selector/key could actually be consumed, and whether the 128 clue selects a known external asset. This tests H1/H3 and avoids pretending 108 bits could supply missing JPEG entropy.
3. **Original print-index/JS and site snapshots:** map endpoint accepted types, exact submission grammar, and historical causal sequence, including alleged termination. Test H3 without guessing at dead endpoints.
4. **Time-anchored Discord proof chain:** for 2020 background, 2021 acorn solution, 2020/21 cover lever, 2020 macOS, and 2018 Xbox residual numerals, recover primary original messages/artifacts rather than citation loops.
5. **Game binary input consumer census:** reproduce SecretJoystick and printer-related input handlers on versioned retail builds. Discriminate a genuine new code path from existing known lever combination or cut logic (H4).
6. **Pre-registered consumer-model testing:** after one of the above establishes a target geometry or grammar, run all physically compatible masters and compare to shuffled/null controls. Avoid a solver that hunts attractive words.
7. **Independent 2018 Xbox digit archaeology:** look for a contemporary second-layer discussion and coordinate mapping. Without one, stop H8.
8. **Physical prospective validation (no outreach):** monitor public existing evidence for residue 82 (U4/U5 fork), 94 (tail stack test), and 61/100 (PR #143 six-state split); record only direct photographed claims and sources.

Specific conditional tests: residue 82 slash would eliminate current U5, whereas dot would eliminate its two G5-only U4 counterparts (Experiment 422); residues 61 and 100 split the six-way overlap in PR #143. These are **model-dependent** priorities, not proof the winning completion's meaning is known.

## Research stopping principles

Preserve raw physical observations unchanged. Prefer discoveries about **what existing artifact would recognize the answer** over another unrestricted cipher sweep.
