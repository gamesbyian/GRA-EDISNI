## Evidence hierarchy and unresolved acquisition

1. **Direct physical facts:** canonical 84-record CSV, original high-res sticker photographs, repeat agreements; distinguish direct photo confirmation from inferred A-I background.
2. **Documented historical mechanisms:** Xbox/iOS/Switch/PC printer arrangement, website submission/returned PDF, CE nine-background image assembly; later write-ups must be traced to original captures/messages to count as independent.
3. **Known unsolved artifacts:** damaged `534brn...` image/page, advertised reversible-cover clue, Xbox residual Braille-digit field. These are evidence of unresolved objects, **not proof** that the foreground encodes their solution.
4. **Candidate hypotheses:** nine-digit ternary mapping, lever symbol commands, generative image seed, external status key, physical/manufacturing routing, terminal 100; all conditional until an external consumer/registration is found.

**Specific gaps:** original fully intact Terminal41 JPEG, reliable complete archived site request/response behavior, original cover image registration to printer assets, authenticated Discord messages not preserved in export, unused game asset census, complete fork diffs/branches, systematic multilingual original-source search, and contemporaneous physical manufacturing specifications. None was silently assumed complete.

Its only contiguous `[A-Za-z0-9+]` run at least 35 characters long is the seven-field 75-character record analyzed in `docs/sticker-endgame-saf-dat-seven-field-audit-2026-10-08.md`.
